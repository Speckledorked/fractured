# Paranoid

## Original Text (from Volume 2: Trait & Flaw Library)

✦ ParanoidType: Situational Description: Sees threats where others don’t—but is right more often than not. Gameplay Effect: Can detect hidden agendas or traps (+1) at the cost of social friction. Triggers / Risk Factors: Trust issues, isolation, false positives. Evolution Path: “Dark Prophet” or “Saboteur”

---

## Mechanics (Unified Tension-Based System)

**Type:** Trait

**Trigger Condition:**  
When **Paranoid** meaningfully impacts the scene or pushes the character toward action, risk, or complication in a way consistent with the description above, the GM may call for a **Trigger Roll**.

**Trigger Roll:**  
Roll **2d6 + the most relevant stat** (often Sanity, Survival, Stamina, Empathy, or Diplomacy, depending on how Paranoid shows up in the moment).

### On a 10+ – Controlled Response  
The character channels Paranoid in a focused, grounded way. They keep control of their behavior and gain a clear benefit from this trait in the fiction.

### On a 7–9 – Strained Response  
The trait helps, but at a cost. The character succeeds or holds together, but the GM introduces a complication, strained relationship, resource hit, or threat escalation tied to how Paranoid manifests.

### On a 6– – Breaking Response  
The trait overwhelms the character. They act too strongly, too late, or in the wrong way. The GM makes a **Hard Move** and immediately applies the Spiral Path below.

---

### Spiral Path (Mechanical Effects)

When Paranoid spirals (typically on a 6– Trigger Roll):

- For the rest of the scene, the character either rolls with **disadvantage** or takes **–1 forward** on rolls tied to this trait (GM’s choice based on context).  
- Advance the current **Threat Clock by 1 segment**.  
- Immediately hit the character’s **Crisis Profile Escalation Threshold**.  
- The GM frames a moment where Paranoid drives reckless, rigid, or harmful behavior that fits the original description.

---

### Resilience Path (Mechanical Effects)

When the character expresses Paranoid in a healthy, grounded, or growth-oriented way and scores a 10+ on a Trigger Roll at a key moment:

- The character gains **+1 forward** on their next roll where they lean on this trait constructively.  
- Choose one ally in the scene; that ally gains **advantage** on their next relevant roll.  
- Either **reduce the Threat Clock by 1 segment** or cancel one imminent escalation related to this situation.  
- Mark **1 progress** toward *evolving* or refining this trait as part of the character’s long-term Growth Path.

---

### Group Effect (Once per Scene)

Once per scene, when Paranoid clearly helps stabilize, inspire, or orient the group:

- Grant one ally **advantage** on a roll to resist fear, doubt, panic, or despair, **or**  
- Add **+1** to the highest result of a **group check**, **or**  
- Let this character **absorb a mild consequence** (loss of position, minor harm, resource hit, or emotional strain) that would otherwise hit an ally.

Use whichever option best fits the fiction at the moment.
