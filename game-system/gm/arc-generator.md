# Arc Generator Toolkit

## Overview

Modular, pressure-based arc scaffolds that evolve characters and relationships across 1-3 sessions. These are GM-facing templates to create character-driven storylines.

## Arc Structure Template

**Arc Name**: Title
**Setup**: Initiating situation
**Beats**: 2-4 scene progression points
**Character Pressure Focus**: What this tests
**Outcome Possibilities**: How it might resolve

---

## BETRAYAL & TRUST ARCS

### "The One We Left Behind"
**Setup**: Group left someone—NPC or PC—for dead. Weeks later, they return. Changed.

**Beats**:
1. Return scene (feral, cold, weapon-drawn, or pleading)
2. Flashback fragments from other survivors
3. Confrontation: "You chose."

**Pressure**: Trust, guilt, betrayal

**Outcomes**:
- Reintegration with scarred loyalty
- Moral collapse of group unity
- New enemy if rejected again

### "The Blood Debt"
**Setup**: Someone dies. Killer is known. But that person is useful—or loved.

**Beats**:
1. Grieving faction forms
2. Retaliation attempt
3. Reckoning (forgiveness, exile, revenge)

**Pressure**: Justice, utility, forgiveness, grief

**Outcomes**:
- New laws formed
- Killer becomes guarded, shaped, or hunted
- Party splits

### "The Other Group"
**Setup**: Another survivor group—functional, well-supplied—offers to merge, trade, or absorb players.

**Beats**:
1. First contact: friendly, cautious, or manipulative
2. Cultural mismatch (discipline, ideology, leadership)
3. One side offers deal that splits party

**Pressure**: Trust, identity, submission vs independence

**Outcomes**:
- One PC defects
- Alliance, war, or silent sabotage
- Collapse from within over who "deserves" survival

## COLLAPSE & BREAKDOWN ARCS

### "The Slow Burn"
**Setup**: One PC begins psychological collapse. Subtle—but growing.

**Beats**:
1. Missed details, shaky aim, mood swings
2. Conflict or hallucination at critical moment
3. Breakdown or violent misstep

**Pressure**: Mental health, burden, "strongest becomes weakest"

**Outcomes**:
- Others carry them forward
- Forced rest or exile
- Redemption through breakdown

### "The Leadership Vacuum"
**Setup**: Key leader (PC or NPC) removed—death, injury, departure. Someone must step up.

**Beats**:
1. First real test post-vacuum
2. Competing styles: hard vs soft
3. Loyalty fracture or unexpected rise

**Pressure**: Authority, fear, charisma, resentment

**Outcomes**:
- New leader forged
- Leadership rotates—chaos or democracy
- Power grab occurs

### "The Divergence"
**Setup**: Two clear paths emerge: one practical and cruel, one noble but dangerous. Party must choose—publicly.

**Beats**:
1. Presentation of both options
2. Debate turns personal
3. Group fractures, votes, or splits

**Pressure**: Leadership, moral compass, ego

**Outcomes**:
- Group divides—temporarily or permanently
- Wrong path chosen, must adapt
- One person changes nature to follow path they swore against

## DISCOVERY & TRUTH ARCS

### "The Message That Changes Everything"
**Setup**: Broken radio or device picks up broadcast. Survivor hears something personal.

**Beats**:
1. "Was that... her voice?"
2. Mission: trace source (real or red herring)
3. Discovery (lie, truth, or trap)

**Pressure**: Hope vs logic, fixation, leadership breakdown

**Outcomes**:
- Leads to new region or risk
- Splits group over what's real
- Emotional closure or destruction

### "The Archive"
**Setup**: Record-keeping site found—video logs, journals, survivor stories. Among them: familiar name.

**Beats**:
1. Record reveals truth one PC didn't know—or didn't want others to
2. Public fallout: exposure, grief, vindication
3. Decision: burn it, broadcast it, or walk away

**Pressure**: Identity, truth, legacy

**Outcomes**:
- PC loses or reclaims power
- Group realigns based on truth revealed
- NPC faction comes seeking archive

### "The Ashes of the Lie"
**Setup**: Core belief—something group or PC built identity on—revealed false.

**Beats**:
1. Seeds of doubt
2. Confirmation: they were wrong
3. Reckoning: continue living lie, or burn it down

**Pressure**: Faith, ideology, self-worth

**Outcomes**:
- PC crumbles or rebuilds as someone new
- Group divides over what truth means
- NPCs exploit fracture—or follow rebuild

## SACRIFICE & IDENTITY ARCS

### "The Pact"
**Setup**: Dying character (PC or NPC) makes another promise to do something if they don't make it.

**Beats**:
1. Promise (revenge, protection, truth delivery)
2. Death
3. Fulfillment—or failure—of pact

**Pressure**: Duty vs survival, memory vs practicality

**Outcomes**:
- Character shifts identity to fulfill promise
- Lie revealed (pact based on false info)
- World changes based on whether pact honored

### "The One Life for All"
**Setup**: Scenario emerges where one character's death, blood, or sacrifice would save everyone else.

**Beats**:
1. Cost becomes clear
2. Group debates: who decides?
3. Final moment: volunteered, forced, or faked

**Pressure**: Martyrdom, agency, love

**Outcomes**:
- They do it. World mourns.
- They fake it—guilt is worse
- They refuse—something else dies in their place

### "The Reclaimer Arc"
**Setup**: Breaking point—emotional, identity-based, or moral. Character must redefine self.

**Beats**:
1. Quiet self-reflection, purposeful ritual
2. Sharp pivot in behavior ("That version of me died")
3. Takes on new vow, mantra, or identity

**Pressure**: Transformation, identity, rebirth

**Outcomes**:
- Becomes beacon of new meaning
- Alienates old bonds while creating new
- Catalyst for group-wide change

## LEGACY & ENDGAME ARCS

### "The Last Lie They Told"
**Setup**: Trusted NPC is dying. Final words contradict everything they said before.

**Beats**:
1. Deathbed confession
2. Players re-evaluate every past choice involving that person
3. Fallout: gift, truth, or final manipulation?

**Pressure**: Legacy, betrayal, purpose

**Outcomes**:
- Group undoes something already sacrificed for
- Confession reveals hidden path—or curse
- PC begins acting like NPC—to fix or finish it

### "The Relic of the World Before"
**Setup**: Something ancient, beautiful, powerful found—untouched by collapse.

**Beats**:
1. Awe and nostalgia
2. Decision: preserve, take, or destroy
3. Moral divide over what legacy means

**Pressure**: History, identity, loss

**Outcomes**:
- Group feels united... or tempted
- One person keeps it, slowly changes them
- NPCs covet it, come to take it

### "The Red Line" (Ultimate Arc)
**Setup**: Group forced into moment where someone must cross personal line—or someone else pays cost.

**Beats**:
1. Impossible decision (kill child, betray friend, destroy haven)
2. Group watches—no one can do it except them
3. Moment: cross it... or change story

**Pressure**: Identity, sacrifice, limits

**Outcomes**:
- PC changes forever
- Group shields them—or can't forgive
- Someone else takes choice from them

## Using Arc Generators

### Selection
- Choose arc that fits current group state
- Match to character crisis profiles
- Consider Season pacing
- Look for natural entry points

### Customization
- Adapt beats to your campaign
- Change NPCs to fit your story
- Modify pressure focus for characters
- Combine arcs for complexity

### Pacing
- 1-session arcs: Use 2 beats
- 2-3 session arcs: Use full beats
- Multi-arc campaigns: Layer arcs

### Resolution
- Don't force specific outcomes
- Let player choices determine direction
- Make all outcomes meaningful
- Connect resolution to larger story

These are scaffolds, not scripts. Follow the fiction.
