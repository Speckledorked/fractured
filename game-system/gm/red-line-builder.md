# Red Line Builder: GM Reference

## 5-Step Scene Template

Use this to craft pressure scenes that test characters' deepest boundaries.

---

## STEP 1: Identify the Line

**What will they not do—even if it costs them everything?**

**Sources:**
- Player's Red Line Declaration (Session Zero)
- Observed behavior patterns
- Stated values in roleplay
- Trait/flaw connections

**Examples:**
- "I never abandon people"
- "I won't kill a child"
- "I won't become like my father"
- "I don't torture. I don't lie."

---

## STEP 2: Invert the Stakes

**Why does breaking the Red Line seem like the only way to save something greater?**

Design scene where "wrong" choice feels justified—even heroic.

**Examples:**
- Killing one traitor saves dozens
- Leaving someone behind protects group
- Becoming monster prevents worse horrors
- Lying saves lives

**Key**: Make both options feel real and justified.

---

## STEP 3: Deny Easy Outs

**Remove third options. Yes or no. Now or never. You or them.**

**Methods:**
- Limit time (30 seconds to decide)
- Remove support (only they can act)
- Make world move faster than intervention
- Create physical barriers to alternatives

**GM Check**: If player finds third option through genuine creativity—consider allowing it. But close obvious loopholes.

---

## STEP 4: Frame the Moment

**Use short, sensory detail. Let emotion rise. Then ask the character, not the player.**

**Template:**
> "[Sensory detail]. [Their name] is [doing what]. [Time pressure]. What do you do?"

**Example:**
> "He's looking at you, whispering your name. The door is closing. The infected are ten feet away. Do you shut the door?"

**Then: STOP NARRATING. Let table breathe.**

---

## STEP 5: Echo the Aftermath

**What changes—even if they "succeeded"?**

**Choose one or more:**
- NPC relationship altered forever
- Morale or reputation penalty
- PC gains new trait or flaw
- Something they say later no longer sounds true
- Group sees them differently
- They see themselves differently

**Don't punish the choice—show its cost.**

---

## Scene Tags

Use tags to categorize Red Line type:

**[🧍 Identity]**: "If I do this, I'm not me anymore"
**[💔 Loyalty]**: "If I do this, I betray who I care about"
**[⚖️ Morality]**: "If I do this, I become the monster"
**[🕊 Humanity]**: "If I do this, I stop being human"
**[⛓ Control]**: "If I do this, I prove I have no choice left"
**[🪞 Belief]**: "If I do this, I was wrong about everything"

## Pre-Built Red Line Prompts

### "Blood for Shelter"
**Tags**: [⚖️ Morality] [💔 Loyalty]
**Setup**: Group offered shelter—only if one person left outside.
**Line Tested**: "I won't abandon people"
**Inversion**: Leaving one saves four
**Denial**: 30 seconds to decide, no negotiation, only four fit
**Moment**: "The door is closing. [Name] outside. Your hand on handle. What do you do?"

### "He Begged Me Not To"
**Tags**: [💔 Loyalty] [🪞 Belief]
**Setup**: Friend begs PC not to kill infected loved one. Others demand it.
**Line Tested**: "I won't hurt people I love" / "I trust my friend's judgment"
**Inversion**: Infected worsening fast, will kill everyone
**Denial**: PC only one with weapon, seconds to decide
**Moment**: "They're changing. Your friend is begging. The group is shouting. You have the gun. What do you do?"

### "One Shot. Not Both."
**Tags**: [⚖️ Morality] [⛓ Control]
**Setup**: Two allies trapped. PC has one bullet.
**Line Tested**: "I won't choose who lives and who dies"
**Inversion**: One can be saved. Both die if no choice made.
**Denial**: Debris falling, fire closing in, one bullet
**Moment**: "You can save one. Choose now. Who?"

### "The Cure Needs a Killer"
**Tags**: [🪞 Belief] [🧍 Identity]
**Setup**: Life-saving treatment requires blood from unwilling innocent.
**Line Tested**: "I won't kill innocents"
**Inversion**: Dozens saved, only one dies
**Denial**: No alternative source, person won't consent, time running out
**Moment**: "Medical expert says: 'We can save them all. But they'll die.' You're holding them down. What do you do?"

### "The Child Saw Everything"
**Tags**: [🕊 Humanity] [💔 Loyalty]
**Setup**: Child NPC witnessed group do something horrific. Beginning to tell others.
**Line Tested**: "I won't hurt children"
**Inversion**: Child talking will unravel group, cause deaths
**Denial**: No way to relocate child, group demands solution, tensions rising
**Moment**: "Child is alone with you. No witnesses. Group expects this problem solved. What do you do?"

## GM Pressure Prompts

**Use these questions when designing Red Line moment:**

**Internal Cost:**
- What does this cost the PC that no one else sees?
- How will they justify this to themselves?
- What part of them dies if they do this?

**External Impact:**
- Who else wanted them to break it—or needed them to hold?
- What happens if they don't break it?
- Who watches? Who knows? Who judges?

**Alternative Choice:**
- What if they don't break it—and someone else does?
- What if they find third way?
- What if they break but differently than expected?

---

## Frequency & Timing

**How Often:**
- Once per major arc (every 3-5 sessions)
- Once per Season recommended
- Maximum 1-2 per character per campaign

**When:**
- After character arc builds pressure
- During Season climaxes
- When thematic moment aligns
- Never randomly—always meaningful

**Red Lines should be rare enough to shock, powerful enough to transform.**

---

## After the Red Line

### If Broken
- Grant bonus Growth Mark
- Apply new trait/flaw reflecting change
- Show reactions from NPCs/PCs
- Let guilt or liberation emerge
- Create echoes in future scenes

### If Held
- Grant bonus Growth Mark
- Show cost of holding line
- Reveal consequences of alternative
- Let others react to refusal
- Make holding matter narratively

### Either Way
- Don't rush to next scene
- Allow processing time
- Show aftermath
- Connect to arcs
- Never make it cheap

---

## Common Mistakes

**Mistake**: Using Red Lines too often
**Fix**: Save for moments that matter most

**Mistake**: Punishing player choice
**Fix**: Make both options have weight, not judgment

**Mistake**: Hidden stakes
**Fix**: Make consequences clear before moment

**Mistake**: Forcing specific outcome
**Fix**: Honor player agency, follow their choice

**Mistake**: Ignoring aftermath
**Fix**: Show long-term impact, let it echo
