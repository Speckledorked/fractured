# Fractured: Quickstart Reference

## Core Concept

Fractured is a narrative-driven survival horror RPG where characters don't just survive—they transform under pressure. Every choice matters. Every failure creates story. Every character changes.

---

## Character Basics

### The 8 Stats (0-5 scale)
- **Health**: Physical resilience
- **Stamina**: Endurance and energy
- **Sanity**: Mental resilience
- **Survival**: Navigation and adaptability
- **Diplomacy**: Persuasion and empathy
- **Engineering**: Repair and building
- **Medical**: Field medicine
- **Combat**: Fighting ability

### Traits & Flaws
- **Traits**: Strengths that provide bonuses
- **Flaws**: Vulnerabilities that complicate

### Red Line
Your moral/emotional boundary: "I will never..."

---

## Core Resolution

### Rolling 2d6 + Stat

**10+**: Success—you achieve your goal
**7-9**: Mixed Success—you succeed, but with cost
**6-**: Failure—the GM makes a hard move

**Modifiers**: +1 for advantage, -1 for disadvantage

---

## Combat Quick Rules

**Taking Action:**
1. Declare what you're doing
2. GM calls for roll if uncertain
3. Roll 2d6 + Combat (or relevant stat)
4. Resolve outcome

**Damage:** Narrative, not hit points
- Minor wounds: -1 to physical actions
- Serious wounds: -2, needs medical attention
- Critical wounds: Life-threatening, immediate care needed

**No initiative**—act when it makes sense narratively

---

## Resources (0-10 scale)

Track these 7 categories:
1. **Medical**: First aid, trauma care
2. **Ammo**: Ammunition and weapon upkeep
3. **Fuel**: Gas, generators, power
4. **Food/Water**: Nutrition and hydration
5. **Utility**: Tools, batteries, radios
6. **Sanity/Comfort**: Morale boosters
7. **Construction**: Building materials

**Mission costs**: Subtract based on team size and mission type
**Scavenging gains**: Add based on haul size
**At 0**: Resource crisis emerges

## Progression

### Growth Marks
Answer reflection questions at session end:
1. Did you act according to your values—even when costly?
2. Did you meaningfully influence another character?
3. Did you face fear/hardship and grow from it?
4. (Bonus) Did you take personal risk for someone else?

**Each "yes" = 1 Growth Mark**
**3 Growth Marks = 1 Character Advance**

### Advances
Choose one:
- Stat Improvement (+1, max 5)
- Trait Evolution (upgrade existing trait)
- Flaw Breakthrough (resolve or transform flaw)
- New Trait (gain GM-offered trait)
- Arc Milestone (resolve backstory element)

## GM Essentials

### GM Moves (Mixed Success 7-9)
- Escalate danger
- Extract cost
- Create urgency
- Force choice
- Reveal unwelcome truth
- Split attention

### GM Moves (Failure 6-)
- Inflict harm
- Destroy equipment
- Drain resources
- Advance threat clock
- Trigger flaw/crisis
- Offer hard choice

### Threat Clocks
- 4 segments: Fast threats
- 6 segments: Building threats
- Advance on failed rolls, risky actions, time passing
- When full: Threat manifests completely

## Session Flow

**1. Opening Frame**
- Establish situation
- Present immediate pressure or calm
- Ask: "What do you do?"

**2. Action & Resolution**
- Players declare actions
- Call for rolls when uncertain
- Use GM moves on mixed/failed rolls
- Advance threat clocks

**3. Crisis Frames**
- Combat, social conflict, survival challenges
- No turn order—narrative flow
- Escalate until resolved

**4. Downtime**
- Recovery and planning
- Character moments
- Processing trauma
- Relationship building

**5. Session End**
- Growth Mark reflection
- Debrief major moments
- Set up next session hook

## First Session Checklist

**Character Creation:**
- [ ] Complete 100-question quiz
- [ ] GM assigns stats (16-20 total points)
- [ ] Assign 2-3 traits, 1-2 flaws
- [ ] Declare Red Line
- [ ] Establish relationships

**World Setup:**
- [ ] Establish starting location
- [ ] Introduce immediate threat
- [ ] Set resource starting levels (4-6 each)
- [ ] Frame Season 1 collapse event

**First Scene:**
- [ ] Drop characters into action
- [ ] Show world state
- [ ] Force immediate choice
- [ ] Establish tone: tension-based, character-focused

## Key Principles

**For Players:**
- Play your character authentically
- Make choices that matter to you
- Lean into your flaws
- Let pressure reveal who you are
- Accept consequences

**For GMs:**
- Follow the fiction
- Make failure interesting
- Telegraph danger
- Honor player agency
- Escalate pressure steadily

**For Everyone:**
- Characters change through play
- Failure creates story
- Moral choices have weight
- Survival isn't guaranteed
- Transformation is the point

## Campaign Structure: Four Seasons

**Season 1: The Break** (Sessions 1-5)
- World collapses
- First Red Line moments
- Establish group bonds

**Season 2: The Divide** (Sessions 6-10)
- Resources scarce
- Internal conflicts emerge
- Moral lines blur

**Season 3: The Fall** (Sessions 11-15)
- Safe places gone
- Brutal choices required
- Traits evolve or spiral

**Season 4: The Reckoning** (Sessions 16-20)
- Final decisions
- Arc resolutions
- Permanent changes
- Legacy established

## Common Scenarios

### "We're Out of Ammo"
- Switch to melee/stealth
- Find ammo through scavenging
- Negotiate or flee
- Improvise weapons

### "Someone's Dying"
- Medical roll to stabilize
- Use medical resources
- Make hard choices if resources depleted
- Process trauma afterward

### "The Group Disagrees"
- Let disagreement play out
- Don't force consensus
- Make both positions valid
- Let consequences teach

### "Character Wants to Leave Group"
- Honor player agency
- Create exit scene
- Show consequences for both character and group
- New character integration

## Remember

**Fractured is about:**
- Who you become under pressure
- Choices that cost something
- Stories that hurt and heal
- Characters that transform

**It's not about:**
- Winning or losing
- Optimizing builds
- Hoarding resources
- Avoiding consequences

**The question isn't "Do we survive?"**

**The question is "Who do we become?"**
