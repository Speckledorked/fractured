# Traits Library

## Overview

Traits represent strengths, capabilities, and defining characteristics that give your character mechanical advantages and narrative hooks. Each trait includes evolution paths—ways it can grow into something greater or decay into something dangerous.

## Trait Structure

Each trait includes:
- **Type**: Core, Conditional, Latent, Passive, or Situational
- **Description**: Narrative impact
- **Gameplay Effect**: Mechanical bonus or behavior effect
- **Triggers/Risk Factors**: When it helps or hinders
- **Evolution Paths**: Spiral (decay) and Resilience (growth) options

## Trait Types

**Core**: Provides consistent bonus or narrative advantage
**Conditional**: Activates under specific circumstances
**Latent**: Grows through play based on player choices
**Passive**: Shapes story without giving mechanical bonus
**Situational**: Depends on environment or context

## PSYCHOLOGICAL TRAITS

### Unshakable
**Type**: Core
**Description**: Rarely cracks under pressure. Calm, even in chaos.
**Effect**: +1 to Sanity checks during trauma or horror events.
**Triggers**: May struggle to emotionally connect with others.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Emotional Bottleneck" (can't process feelings)
- Resilience: "Stoic Guardian" (unbreakable center for others)

### Hyperfocused
**Type**: Conditional
**Description**: Can lock in on one problem and burn through it obsessively.
**Effect**: +2 on single-task actions, -1 to peripheral awareness.
**Triggers**: May ignore team needs or threats outside focus.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Tunnel Vision" (misses everything else)
- Resilience: "Master Technician" (precision under pressure)

### Optimist
**Type**: Passive
**Description**: Always finds a silver lining, even when there shouldn't be one.
**Effect**: Reduces group despair penalties by 1 in loss scenarios.
**Triggers**: May become naive or unprepared for worst outcomes.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Broken Idealist" (hope shatters completely)
- Resilience: "Hope-Bearer" (inspires others genuinely)

### Paranoid
**Type**: Situational
**Description**: Sees threats where others don't—but is right more often than not.
**Effect**: Can detect hidden agendas or traps (+1) at cost of social friction.
**Triggers**: Trust issues, isolation, false positives cause conflict.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Saboteur" (preemptive attacks on perceived threats)
- Resilience: "Dark Prophet" (saves group through suspicion)

### Calculating
**Type**: Core
**Description**: Weighs every action. Nothing is random.
**Effect**: Gain reroll once per day on tactical, planning, or resource-based checks.
**Triggers**: Hesitates in spontaneous situations.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Emotionless Planner" (loses humanity for efficiency)
- Resilience: "Strategic Mastermind" (thinks three steps ahead)

### Curious
**Type**: Core
**Description**: Must know how things work. Opens every door.
**Effect**: Bonus clue detection or secret finding (+1).
**Triggers**: Risk-prone, may break protocol to investigate.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Unleashed Catalyst" (curiosity creates disaster)
- Resilience: "Field Scholar" (knowledge becomes power)

### Mindful
**Type**: Passive
**Description**: Practices emotional and sensory awareness. Breathes, observes, responds.
**Effect**: +1 to recovery checks after mental trauma or strain.
**Triggers**: May pause too long in high-pressure sequences.
**Evolution**:

### Adaptive Thinker

**Description**: Thinks fast. Shifts strategies mid-move.

## USING TRAITS IN PLAY

### When Traits Apply
- Player declares action that fits trait description
- GM agrees the trait is relevant
- Apply mechanical bonus
- Describe how the trait manifests

### When Traits Complicate
- Trait creates dilemma (e.g., "Protector" must choose who to save)
- Trait triggers risk (e.g., "Curious" opens wrong door)
- Trait conflicts with group (e.g., "Code-Follower" vs pragmatism)

### Evolution Timing
- After 3+ significant uses in crisis situations
- When Crisis Profile aligns with trait
- Following Red Line moment related to trait
- GM determines character has been tested enough

### Evolution Choice
- Present both spiral and resilience options
- Let player choose direction
- Apply new trait, remove old one
- Traits can evolve multiple times through campaign

## GM NOTES

### Trait Selection
- Choose traits that create story opportunities
- Mix mechanical and narrative traits
- Avoid redundancy with high stats
- Select traits with interesting evolution potential

### Trait Triggers
- Look for moments where traits should matter
- Don't wait for players to invoke traits—offer them
- Use traits to create dramatic moments
- Let traits complicate as well as help

### Trait Evolution
- Track trait usage throughout campaign
- Note which traits are being tested most
- Prepare evolution choices when appropriate
- Make evolution meaningful, not automatic

### Balance
- Not all traits are equally powerful—that's fine
- Story impact matters more than mechanical balance
- Let players discover creative uses for traits
- Reward players who lean into their traits
