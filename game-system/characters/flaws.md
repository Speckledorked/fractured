# Flaws Library

## Overview

Flaws represent vulnerabilities, complications, and defining weaknesses that create tension and roleplay opportunities. Unlike traits, flaws don't just add mechanical penalties—they create narrative pressure and character depth.

## Flaw Structure

Each flaw includes:
- **Type**: Emotional, Behavioral, Social, Cognitive, Physical, or Moral
- **Description**: Narrative impact
- **Gameplay Effect**: Mechanical penalty or complication
- **Triggers/Risk Factors**: When it activates
- **Evolution Paths**: Spiral (worsens) and Resilience (resolves) options

## Flaw Philosophy

**Flaws create story, not frustration.**

- Flaws should complicate decisions, not paralyze play
- Flaws trigger when dramatically appropriate, not constantly
- Flaws can be overcome through roleplay and growth
- Flaws that spiral create permanent character change

## EMOTIONAL FLAWS

### Fear of Failure
**Type**: Emotional
**Description**: Avoids action when success isn't guaranteed. Haunted by letting people down.
**Effect**: Must pass Resolve check to take major risks or leadership roles.
**Triggers**: When stakes feel high or others are watching.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Chronic Avoider" (won't act at all)
- Resilience: "Unlikely Hero" (overcomes fear through action)

### Guilt Magnet
**Type**: Emotional
**Description**: Absorbs responsibility—even when it isn't theirs to carry.
**Effect**: Morale drops faster when civilians or teammates are lost.
**Triggers**: Post-mission reviews, arguments, group fractures.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Self-Punisher" (actively seeks punishment)
- Resilience: "Redeemer" (uses guilt to help others)

### Bottles Everything
**Type**: Psychological
**Description**: Doesn't talk about pain. Doesn't cry. Doesn't process.
**Effect**: Cannot recover Sanity between sessions without specific outlet (writing, talking, etc.).
**Triggers**: After deaths, losses, or arguments.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Explosive Breakdown" (catastrophic collapse)
- Resilience: "Quiet Survivor" (manages emotions privately)

### Validation Addict
**Type**: Emotional
**Description**: Needs to be needed. Needs to be seen.
**Effect**: -1 to resolve or morale if efforts aren't acknowledged.
**Triggers**: Overachieves in visible roles, underperforms when unseen.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Spiteful Shadow" (resents being overlooked)
- Resilience: "Self-Affirming" (finds worth internally)

### Emotional Volatility
**Type**: Behavioral
**Description**: Anger, joy, grief—everything hits like a truck.
**Effect**: May roll with advantage or disadvantage depending on emotional state (GM's call).
**Triggers**: Interpersonal stress, betrayal, isolation.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Emotional Risk Factor" (unpredictable danger)
- Resilience: "Burning Heart" (passion becomes strength)

### Fear of Intimacy
**Type**: Psychological
**Description**: Can fight beside you. Just don't ask them to feel.
**Effect**: -1 on bonding or trust-building checks.
**Triggers**: When asked about past, love, or loss.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Unreachable Wall" (complete isolation)
- Resilience: "Slowly Opening Door" (learns to connect)

### Haunted
**Type**: Psychological
**Description**: Something—or someone—they lost defines their pain.
**Effect**: Random penalty each session based on environment (triggers: names, photos, landmarks).
**Triggers**: Familiar places, child survivors, specific names.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Ghost-Chaser" (obsessed with the dead)
- Resilience: "Vigil Guardian" (honors without being consumed)

### Savior Syndrome
**Type**: Emotional
**Description**: Must save everyone—even if it kills them.
**Effect**: Will not leave behind injured teammates unless forced.
**Triggers**: Rescue ops, retreats, triage situations.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Martyr" (seeks death through saving)
- Resilience: "Selective Protector" (saves wisely)

### Fears Being Forgotten
**Type**: Existential
**Description**: Legacy matters. Survival isn't enough—they need meaning.
**Effect**: Must make public impact each session or suffer morale drop.
**Triggers**: Quiet roles, background assignments.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Self-Destructive Icon" (glory-seeking)
- Resilience: "Symbol of Hope" (earns lasting legacy)

### Abandonment Wound
**Type**: Emotional
**Description**: Left behind before—never forgot it.
**Effect**: Panic or hostility when isolated.
**Triggers**: Splitting parties, silent treatment, exile.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Saboteur of Trust" (destroys before being left)
- Resilience: "Group Glue" (holds others together)

## BEHAVIORAL FLAWS

### Impulse Control
**Type**: Behavioral
**Description**: Acts before thinking. Emotion over strategy.
**Effect**: Must roll to resist temptation, provocation, or sudden risk.
**Triggers**: Tense negotiations, emotional conflict, fear-based events.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Chaos Catalyst" (actions create disasters)
- Resilience: "Hard Lesson" (learns to pause)

### Always the Hero
**Type**: Behavioral
**Description**: Rushes in. Won't let others take hit—even when they should.
**Effect**: Cannot resist intervening when others threatened. +1 fatigue or injury risk.
**Triggers**: Kidnappings, hostage situations, traps.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Fallen Idol" (dies needlessly)
- Resilience: "Beloved Leader" (heroism inspires)

### Distrustful
**Type**: Social-Cognitive
**Description**: Doesn't buy good intentions. Keeps everyone at arm's length.
**Effect**: Slower group cohesion; must earn trust manually.
**Triggers**: Infiltration events, moral arguments, ambiguous loyalties.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Saboteur by Doubt" (paranoia destroys group)
- Resilience: "Vigilant Sentry" (protects through suspicion)

### Needs to Win
**Type**: Behavioral
**Description**: Winning isn't goal—it's survival. Can't lose face.
**Effect**: Will escalate simple disagreements into full contests.
**Triggers**: Power struggles, rival NPCs, leadership challenges.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Broken Tyrant" (obsessed with dominance)
- Resilience: "Alpha Figure" (leads through strength)

### Analysis Paralysis
**Type**: Cognitive
**Description**: Weighs every option too long. Gets stuck in "what ifs."
**Effect**: -1 to reaction time in high-stakes choices; loses tie-breaks.
**Triggers**: Pressure, moral dilemmas, ambiguous data.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Overwhelmed" (can't decide at all)
- Resilience: "Precision Planner" (thinks carefully but acts)

### Chronic Comparer
**Type**: Emotional-Cognitive
**Description**: Always measuring themselves against others—often unfairly.
**Effect**: -1 morale or self-confidence if another player shines in similar role.
**Triggers**: Recognition ceremonies, leader praise, visible skill gaps.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Toxic Underminer" (sabotages others)
- Resilience: "Fighter for Worth" (finds own value)

### Obsessive
**Type**: Behavioral
**Description**: Can't let go of tasks until "perfect"—or ruined.
**Effect**: Cannot exit mission task early without Willpower check.
**Triggers**: Broken plans, shifting priorities.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Cracked Perfectionist" (standards destroy them)
- Resilience: "Master of Detail" (perfectionism becomes skill)

### Contrarian
**Type**: Social-Cognitive
**Description**: Always takes opposing stance—on principle.
**Effect**: +1 resistance to persuasion, but hinders group cohesion.
**Triggers**: Planning sessions, group votes, authority calls.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Outcast" (rejected by group)
- Resilience: "Rebel Thinker" (challenges make group stronger)

### Memory Triggers
**Type**: Cognitive-Trauma
**Description**: Certain sights, smells, sounds can lock them in past.
**Effect**: GM may call for freeze or Sanity test when sensory triggers appear.
**Triggers**: Environmental echoes of pre-collapse life.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Emotional Prisoner" (trapped in past)
- Resilience: "Resilient Rememberer" (honors past, lives present)

### One-Track Mind
**Type**: Cognitive
**Description**: If they believe they're right, nothing gets through.
**Effect**: +1 on focused tasks, -2 to adapting when things change mid-action.
**Triggers**: Command redirections, mission curveballs, betrayal.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Unyielding Stubborn" (rigidity causes failure)
- Resilience: "Unstoppable Force" (focus becomes power)

## VICE & DEPENDENCY FLAWS

### Alcohol Dependent
**Type**: Addiction
**Description**: Uses alcohol to numb pain or survive the day.
**Effect**: Suffers -1 to morale or Sanity if unable to drink at least once per day.
**Triggers**: Withdrawal, dry camps, rationing.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Functional Addict" (needs it to function)
- Resilience: "Clean and Shaking" (overcomes addiction)

### Adrenaline Junkie
**Type**: Behavioral Dependency
**Description**: Feels alive only when it's life or death.
**Effect**: Morale and bonuses fade during calm or peaceful scenes.
**Triggers**: Recovery periods, long stretches of quiet.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Burnout Crash" (seeks death through danger)
- Resilience: "Hero of Edge" (channels need productively)

### Overeater
**Type**: Stress Coping
**Description**: Consumes more than needed—when available.
**Effect**: Uses 1.5x rations per downtime unless restrained.
**Triggers**: Stress, grief, overabundant supply.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Resource Drain" (group suffers for it)
- Resilience: "Comfort Seeker" (finds other outlets)

### Pain Pill Dependent
**Type**: Chemical
**Description**: Built tolerance—or addiction—to numbing agents.
**Effect**: -1 to Endurance and Willpower if unable to access meds every 48 hrs.
**Triggers**: Injury, field missions, destroyed med storage.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Fiend" (will do anything for next dose)
- Resilience: "Managed Crutch" (controls dependency)

### Workaholic
**Type**: Psychological
**Description**: Can't stop doing. Can't sit still.
**Effect**: Penalized recovery unless given tasks even during downtime.
**Triggers**: Forced rest, orders to pause.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Exhausted Leader" (collapses from overwork)
- Resilience: "Camp Pillar" (work sustains group)

### Self-Harmer
**Type**: Emotional
**Description**: Pain regulates them when nothing else does.
**Effect**: May reduce Sanity loss but takes HP damage to do so.
**Triggers**: Grief, helplessness, failure.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Cycle of Hurt" (escalates damage)
- Resilience: "Scarred Survivor" (finds healthier outlets)

### Gambling Instinct
**Type**: Psychological
**Description**: Will always bet on risk—even when stakes are survival.
**Effect**: Will ignore odds if big gain is present.
**Triggers**: Loot choices, strategic votes, resource trades.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Cost of One Roll" (loses everything)
- Resilience: "Fortune's Hand" (learns when to bet)

## DARKNESS-TEMPTED FLAWS

### Ruthless
**Type**: Moral
**Description**: Will do whatever it takes. No hesitation.
**Effect**: +1 to intimidation or tactical efficiency; -1 morale in allies after witnessing brutality.
**Triggers**: Prisoners, surrender scenarios, wounded civilians.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Lone Wolf Outcast" (group rejects them)
- Resilience: "Necessary Monster" (group understands necessity)

### Secret Sadist
**Type**: Psychological
**Description**: Part of them enjoys the violence.
**Effect**: May gain temporary bonuses after harming others, even enemies.
**Triggers**: Close-quarters combat, interrogations.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Slaughter Machine" (loses humanity completely)
- Resilience: "Blood Angel" (controls dark urges)

### Weapon Obsessed
**Type**: Behavioral
**Description**: Feels powerful through weapons—stockpiles, over-arms, overkills.
**Effect**: -1 penalty to disarmament diplomacy; +1 with arms checks.
**Triggers**: Disarm orders, leadership shifts, civilian zones.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Gun Ghoul" (guns define identity)
- Resilience: "Walking Arsenal" (prepared for anything)

### Ideologue
**Type**: Moral Rigidity
**Description**: Believes in worldview above all—even survival.
**Effect**: Can't compromise on core ideals.
**Triggers**: Faction politics, ethics crises, diplomacy.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Fascist Flame" (ideology becomes tyranny)
- Resilience: "Beacon of Fire" (inspires through belief)

### Power Hungry
**Type**: Social-Moral
**Description**: Craves leadership, dominance, and control.
**Effect**: Will attempt to seize control if leadership appears weak.
**Triggers**: Leader stumbles, elections, crisis of command.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Ruthless Usurper" (takes power by force)
- Resilience: "Strong Hand" (leads effectively)

### Secret Collaborator
**Type**: Betrayal
**Description**: Once worked with enemy. Still might be.
**Effect**: NPCs or factions may recognize or expose this connection.
**Triggers**: Recon, prison rescues, memory leaks.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Rot From Within" (active betrayal)
- Resilience: "Redeemed Spy" (earns trust back)

### Glory Chaser
**Type**: Narcissist
**Description**: Wants to be remembered—not just survive.
**Effect**: +1 when others watching; -1 when unseen or forgotten.
**Triggers**: Solo missions, routine duties, loss of spotlight.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Flameout Star" (dies seeking glory)
- Resilience: "Legend" (earns genuine fame)

## SOCIAL & PHYSICAL FLAWS

### Bad Liar
**Type**: Social
**Description**: Can't bluff. It's all written on their face.
**Effect**: -2 to any deception unless coached.
**Triggers**: Interrogations, spy games.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Panicked Faker" (lies make things worse)
- Resilience: "Honest to Death" (honesty becomes strength)

### Claustrophobic
**Type**: Phobia
**Description**: Tight spaces trigger full-blown panic.
**Effect**: Must roll Resolve to enter vents, basements, or crawlspaces.
**Triggers**: Collapsed buildings, bunkers.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Sealed Fate" (panic kills them)
- Resilience: "Tunnel Brave" (overcomes fear)

### Visibly Scarred
**Type**: Social-Physical
**Description**: Has mark, disfigurement, or injury that draws attention.
**Effect**: -1 to persuasion in tense diplomacy, +1 to intimidation.
**Triggers**: Civilian camps, mirror moments.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Marked Pariah" (rejected by communities)
- Resilience: "Wounded Survivor" (scars tell story of survival)

### Illiterate
**Type**: Knowledge
**Description**: Can't read—maybe never could.
**Effect**: Cannot access books, written maps, signs, or coded warnings without aid.
**Triggers**: Complex tech, written instructions.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Manipulated Tool" (used by others)
- Resilience: "Adapted Learner" (finds other ways)

### Hunted
**Type**: Narrative
**Description**: Someone—or something—is actively tracking them.
**Effect**: GM may trigger threat events tied specifically to this player.
**Triggers**: Camp exposure, recon trips.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Hunted to Death" (caught eventually)
- Resilience: "Stalker's Bane" (turns tables on hunter)

### Head Injury Survivor
**Type**: Medical
**Description**: Lives with lingering effects from traumatic head wound.
**Effect**: Memory gaps, +1 difficulty on focus checks.
**Triggers**: Loud noises, sudden pressure shifts.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Cracked Lens" (deteriorates mentally)
- Resilience: "Cognitive Rebuilder" (adapts to injury)

### Luckless
**Type**: Cosmic
**Description**: When things go wrong—they go extra wrong.
**Effect**: GM may apply random failures 1x per act, even on good rolls.
**Triggers**: Dice rolls, key moments, overconfidence.
**Evolution**:
- Spiral: "Fated Tragedy" (cursed existence)
- Resilience: "Beats Odds Anyway" (triumphs despite luck)

## USING FLAWS IN PLAY

### When Flaws Trigger

**GM-Triggered:**
- Situation naturally aligns with flaw
- Dramatic moment demands complication
- Character hasn't been challenged recently

**Player-Triggered:**
- Player voluntarily leans into flaw
- Player roleplays flaw without prompting
- Player accepts complication for story

### Flaw Activation

**1. Recognize Trigger**
Scene or situation matches flaw description

**2. Frame the Complication**
> "Your 'Guilt Magnet' flaw activates. You see their face in the survivor's eyes. This one's on you."

**3. Apply Effect**
Mechanical penalty or narrative complication

**4. Let Player Respond**
Allow character choice in how they handle it

### Flaw Spiral Warnings

**First Warning (2-3 triggers):**
Narrative weight increases, no mechanical change
> "The guilt is getting worse. You're not sleeping."

**Second Warning (4-5 triggers):**
Offer choice: confront or spiral
> "Make a Sanity roll to process this, or let it become 'Self-Punisher'"

**Full Spiral (unaddressed):**
Flaw mutates into worse version
Apply new mechanical effect and narrative consequences

### Flaw Breakthrough

**Requirements:**
- Confront flaw source (roleplay + Sanity roll)
- OR spend 3 Growth Marks choosing Breakthrough
- OR Red Line moment forcing reckoning
- OR sustained group support over multiple sessions

**Process:**
1. Create confrontation scene
2. Roll Sanity or relevant stat
   - **10+**: Breakthrough—flaw resolved or transformed
   - **7-9**: Progress—flaw reduces, may need one more push
   - **6-**: Spiral—flaw worsens
3. Apply transformation if successful

**Result:**
- Flaw removed or becomes lesser version
- Character gains new trait reflecting growth
- Permanently changes Crisis Profile

## Flaw Management

### Tracking Flaw Triggers
- Note when flaws activate
- Count towards spiral threshold
- Watch for patterns in character behavior

### Balancing Flaws
- Don't trigger every session—save for impact
- Use flaws to create dilemmas, not frustration
- Reward players who lean into flaws
- Allow breakthrough when earned

### Flaw Evolution
- All flaws should evolve by end of campaign
- Some resolve (breakthrough)
- Some worsen (spiral)
- Some transform (new flaw replaces old)

### Multiple Flaws
- Characters may have 1-2 active flaws
- Don't trigger all flaws simultaneously
- Focus on one at a time for dramatic impact
- Consider relationships between flaws

## GM NOTES

### Flaw Selection
- Choose flaws that create story opportunities
- Avoid flaws that just annoy or paralyze
- Select flaws with clear triggers
- Pick flaws that can evolve interestingly

### Flaw Triggers
- Look for natural moments where flaws matter
- Don't force flaw activations—let them emerge
- Use flaws to complicate, not punish
- Make flaw triggers dramatically appropriate

### Flaw Evolution
- Track flaw activations throughout campaign
- Provide opportunities for breakthrough
- Make spirals feel tragic but earned
- Let players direct flaw arcs when possible

### Balance
- Flaws should create tension, not misery
- Every flaw should have breakthrough potential
- Not all flaws need to spiral—some resolve
- Let flaws shape character arcs meaningfully

Flaws aren't weaknesses to overcome—they're invitations to become someone different.
