# Narrative Progression System

## Philosophy

Fractured rewards emotional depth, meaningful decisions, and character development—not combat victories or loot gathering. Characters grow through facing fears, making hard choices, and staying true to (or breaking from) their values.

## Core Mechanic: Growth Marks

**Growth Marks** are the primary progression currency. They're earned through:
- Acting according to character values (even when costly)
- Influencing others meaningfully
- Facing fears and moral challenges
- Taking personal risks for others

**Every 3 Growth Marks**, a character earns one **Character Advance**.

---

## Growth Marks

### Earning Growth Marks

At the end of each session, the GM leads a reflection. Players answer questions based on their actions. Each "yes" earns 1 Growth Mark.

### Standard Session Reflection Questions

**1. Did you act in accordance with your traits, flaws, or moral code—even when it cost you something?**

Examples of "yes":
- You stayed to fight when fleeing was safer (trait: Protector)
- You refused to lie despite consequences (trait: Honest)
- You took blame because of guilt (flaw: Guilt Magnet)
- You held your Red Line when pressed

**2. Did you influence another character in a meaningful way—through trust, conflict, loyalty, or sacrifice?**

Examples of "yes":
- You talked down a panicking teammate
- You started a necessary but painful argument
- You gave up something to prove your loyalty
- Your sacrifice changed someone's perspective

**3. Did you face a fear, hardship, or moral choice that tested your values—and grow from it, even slightly?**

Examples of "yes":
- You entered tight spaces despite claustrophobia
- You made hard choice between two bad options
- You confronted trauma instead of avoiding it
- You chose difficult right over easy wrong

**4. (Optional Bonus) Did you take a personal risk for someone else's survival, well-being, or hope?**

Examples of "yes":
- You gave up rations to feed another
- You volunteered for dangerous mission to protect teammate
- You stayed behind to buy time
- You risked reputation to defend someone

### Maximum Per Session
- Typically 1-3 Growth Marks per session
- Exceptional sessions (Red Line moments, major breakthroughs): up to 4
- Quiet sessions (downtime, recovery): 0-1 is fine

### Tracking Growth Marks
Use three checkboxes:
- [ ] [ ] [ ] Empty = 0 marks
- [X] [ ] [ ] = 1 mark
- [X] [X] [ ] = 2 marks
- [X] [X] [X] = 3 marks (advance now!)

When you take an advance, clear all three boxes and start over.

## Character Advances

Once a player collects 3 Growth Marks, they choose one advance option:

### Advance Options

**1. Stat Improvement**
- Raise one stat by +1 (maximum 5)
- Must be justified by recent in-game experience
- Can't improve stat not used meaningfully

**Requirements**:
- Used stat multiple times during recent sessions
- Made key rolls using that stat
- Succeeded or failed memorably with that stat
- GM agrees improvement makes narrative sense

**Examples**:
- Raise Combat after surviving multiple firefights
- Raise Medical after treating serious injuries under pressure
- Raise Sanity after processing major trauma successfully
- Raise Diplomacy after negotiating critical agreements

**2. Trait Evolution**
- Upgrade existing trait into stronger or refined version
- Must follow spiral or resilience path
- Based on trait's narrative arc

**Requirements**:
- Trait triggered multiple times (3+)
- Character faced crisis related to trait
- Clear direction (spiral or resilience) emerged
- GM agrees evolution is appropriate

**Examples**:
- "Protector" evolves to "Shield of Many" (resilience)
- "Charismatic" evolves to "Manipulator" (spiral)
- "Calculated Risk-Taker" evolves to "Heroic Operator" (resilience)

**3. Flaw Breakthrough or Transformation**
- Resolve a flaw completely
- Transform flaw into evolved version
- Replace flaw with related trait

**Requirements**:
- Confronted flaw source through roleplay
- Made Sanity roll or equivalent check
- Demonstrated growth against flaw
- GM agrees breakthrough earned

**Examples**:
- "Guilt Magnet" resolves into "Redeemer" trait
- "Fear of Failure" transforms to lesser version
- "Savior Syndrome" breakthrough after letting go

**4. New Trait Unlock**
- Gain new trait offered by GM
- Based on recent choices and arc
- Conditional or Latent trait appropriate

**Requirements**:
- Demonstrated new behavior pattern
- Made choices that suggest new trait
- GM sees narrative opening
- Fits character development

**Examples**:
- After multiple successful scavenging: "Efficient Forager"
- After protecting others repeatedly: "Protective Presence"
- After processing trauma healthily: "Mindful"

**5. Arc Milestone**
- Instead of mechanical bonus, resolve major backstory element
- Complete personal mission or mystery
- Close chapter of character history

**Benefits**:
- May unlock future bonuses
- Affects reputation or faction standing
- Provides emotional closure
- Opens new narrative threads

**Examples**:
- Find out what happened to family
- Resolve guilt over past failure
- Complete revenge or redemption quest
- Discover truth about outbreak origin

## Advance Guidelines

### Stat Improvement Limits
- Maximum stat value: 5
- No more than one stat improved per advance
- Can't raise same stat twice in row (usually)
- Must justify through recent play

### Trait Evolution Limits
- Can only evolve traits you have
- Evolution must follow established path
- Can't evolve same trait multiple times rapidly
- Some traits have multiple evolution stages

### Multiple Advances
Over a campaign, characters typically earn:
- **Season 1** (4-5 sessions): 1-2 advances
- **Season 2** (4-5 sessions): 2-3 advances
- **Season 3** (4-5 sessions): 2-3 advances
- **Season 4** (4-5 sessions): 1-2 advances
- **Total**: 6-10 advances per campaign

### Pacing
- Too fast: Progression feels cheap
- Too slow: Players feel stagnant
- Right pace: 1 advance every 2-3 sessions
- Adjust based on session intensity and length

## Optional: Bonus Growth Marks

### GM-Awarded Bonuses
Award extra Growth Mark for:
- **Red Line moments**: Crossing or holding Red Line
- **Breakthrough moments**: Major character transformation
- **Selfless sacrifice**: True heroism without seeking reward
- **Exceptional roleplay**: Deeply moving or powerful moment

Award at GM discretion, maximum 1 per session.

### Player-Nominated Bonuses
Allow players to nominate one other player per session for bonus Growth Mark:
- Nominated for emotional impact
- Nominated for breakthrough moment
- Nominated for exceptional character work
- Voted on by group if desired

Creates positive reinforcement and encourages recognition.

### Session Zero Bonuses
Consider awarding 1-2 Growth Marks during Session Zero for:
- Completing character creation thoroughly
- Declaring Red Line
- Establishing relationships
- Creating detailed backstory

Gets players started with progression.

## Integration with Other Systems

### Growth Marks + Crisis Engine
- Failed Sanity rolls don't prevent Growth Marks
- Spiraling flaws don't negate progression
- Crisis transformation can earn Growth Marks
- Character changes earn advancement

### Growth Marks + Traits/Flaws
- Can't advance traits without Growth Marks
- Can't breakthrough flaws without Growth Marks
- Traits evolved through advances stay evolved
- Flaws resolved through advances stay resolved

### Growth Marks + Red Lines
- Red Line moments often earn bonus marks
- Crossing Red Line doesn't prevent advancement
- Holding Red Line rewards growth
- Red Line scenes create advance opportunities

### Growth Marks + Seasons
- Track total advances per season
- Some advances matter more in certain seasons
- Campaign arc shapes advance timing
- Seasons provide natural advancement checkpoints

### Reflection Process
**End of session (5-10 minutes):**
1. Read each reflection question aloud
2. Give players time to consider
3. Go around table, each answers
4. Mark Growth Marks for "yes" answers
5. Congratulate any advances earned

### Offering Advances
When player reaches 3 marks:
1. List available options
2. Explain requirements for each
3. Discuss what fits character
4. Let player choose
5. Apply advance immediately
6. Reset marks to zero

### Balancing Group
If one player advances faster:
- That's okay—different characters, different arcs
- Ensure slower players still get spotlight
- Don't artificially slow anyone down
- Growth isn't competition

### Difficult Players
If player never earns marks:
- Check if questions fit their playstyle
- Discuss what kind of moments matter to them
- Offer alternative reflection questions
- Some players prefer mechanical focus—that's okay

## Alternative Reflection Questions

### For Combat-Focused Characters
1. Did you protect others in combat at personal risk?
2. Did you make tactical decision that saved lives?
3. Did you show mercy or restraint when violence was easier?

### For Support Characters
1. Did you keep someone alive through your actions?
2. Did you maintain morale when others were breaking?
3. Did you sacrifice resources to help another?

### For Lone Wolves
1. Did you act against your isolationist nature to help group?
2. Did you reveal vulnerability to another character?
3. Did you let someone in despite your walls?

### For Pragmatists
1. Did you make hard choice others couldn't make?
2. Did you balance efficiency with humanity?
3. Did you bear burden of leadership?

### Customize questions to fit your table's focus and character types.

## Example Progression

**Maya, Combat 2, Medical 4, Sanity 2**

**Session 1-2**: Earns 2 Growth Marks for treating wounded under fire and facing trauma of losing patient.

**Session 3**: Earns 1 Growth Mark for staying calm when team panics. **Total: 3 marks.**
- **Takes Advance**: Raises Medical to 5 (has repeatedly done critical medical work)
- Resets to 0 marks

**Session 4-6**: Earns 3 Growth Marks for holding Red Line (won't let others die for her), processing grief, and protecting teammate.
- **Takes Advance**: Evolves "Protector" trait to "Guardian Angel" (resilience path)
- Resets to 0 marks

**Session 7-8**: Earns 2 Growth Marks for making hard triage decision and confronting "Guilt Magnet" flaw.

**Session 9**: Earns 1 Growth Mark plus 1 bonus for breakthrough moment where she forgives herself. **Total: 4 marks** (saves 1 for next advance).

**Session 10**: Earns 1 Growth Mark. **Total: 4 marks** (over 3).
- **Takes Advance**: Flaw Breakthrough—"Guilt Magnet" transforms into "Redeemer" trait
- 1 mark carries over

**By Campaign End**: 4-5 advances total. Higher stats, evolved traits, resolved flaw, transformed character.

## Growth Marks Philosophy

**Growth Marks reward:**
- Playing your character, not optimizing
- Making hard choices, not easy ones
- Staying true to values, not abandoning them
- Risking yourself, not just surviving

**Growth Marks create:**
- Investment in character arc
- Focus on meaningful moments
- Recognition of emotional work
- Mechanical reward for roleplay

**Growth Marks are not:**
- Participation trophies
- Automatic per session
- Based on success/failure
- Combat or loot dependent

They measure what matters: who you become when everything is at risk.
