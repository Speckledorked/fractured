# Growth Marks Reference

## Quick Overview

**Growth Marks** are earned at the end of each session by answering reflection questions. Collect 3 marks, earn 1 Character Advance.

---

## Session Reflection Questions

Answer "yes" or "no" to each question. Each "yes" earns 1 Growth Mark.

### Core Questions

**1. Character Integrity**
> Did you act in accordance with your traits, flaws, or moral code—even when it cost you something?

**2. Meaningful Influence**
> Did you influence another character in a meaningful way—through trust, conflict, loyalty, or sacrifice?

**3. Facing Challenges**
> Did you face a fear, hardship, or moral choice that tested your values—and grow from it, even slightly?

**4. Personal Risk (Bonus)**
> Did you take a personal risk for someone else's survival, well-being, or hope?

---

## Tracking

Use simple checkbox tracker:

```
Growth Marks: [ ] [ ] [ ]
```

- Empty box = not yet earned
- Filled box = earned this session or carried over
- Three filled = time to advance!

After taking an advance, reset all boxes to empty.

## Tips for Players

### "Yes" Examples

**Character Integrity:**
- Stayed to fight when fleeing was safer (Protector trait)
- Refused to lie despite consequences (Honest trait)
- Took blame due to guilt (Guilt Magnet flaw)
- Held your Red Line when tested

**Meaningful Influence:**
- Talked down panicking teammate
- Started necessary but painful argument
- Gave up something to prove loyalty
- Changed someone's perspective through sacrifice

**Facing Challenges:**
- Entered tight spaces despite claustrophobia
- Made choice between two bad options
- Confronted trauma instead of avoiding it
- Chose difficult right over easy wrong

**Personal Risk:**
- Gave up rations to feed another
- Volunteered for dangerous mission to protect teammate
- Stayed behind to buy time
- Risked reputation to defend someone

### "No" Examples
- Acted purely tactically without character moment
- Avoided all risk and challenge
- Made easy or safe choices
- Didn't interact meaningfully with others
- Quiet session with no character development

**Note**: "No" is fine! Not every session has major character moments. Average is 1-2 marks per session.

## Earning Rates

### Typical Session
**1-2 Growth Marks**
- Normal character development
- Some roleplay moments
- Standard moral choices

### Intense Session
**2-3 Growth Marks**
- Major character challenges
- Red Line proximity
- Significant sacrifice or growth

### Exceptional Session
**3-4 Growth Marks**
- Red Line crossed or held
- Major breakthrough moment
- Transformative experience

### Quiet Session
**0-1 Growth Marks**
- Downtime and recovery
- Planning and preparation
- Minimal character challenges

**This is fine**—not every session needs high drama.

---

## Bonus Growth Marks

### GM-Awarded (Optional)
Extra mark for:
- Red Line moment
- Breakthrough moment
- Exceptional roleplay
- Selfless sacrifice

Maximum 1 bonus per session.

### Player-Nominated (Optional)
Players nominate one other player for bonus mark:
- For emotional impact
- For breakthrough moment
- For exceptional character work

Vote or GM decides if awarded.

## Spending Growth Marks

### When You Reach 3 Marks
Choose one Character Advance:

1. **Stat Improvement** (+1 to any stat, max 5)
2. **Trait Evolution** (upgrade existing trait)
3. **Flaw Breakthrough** (resolve or transform flaw)
4. **New Trait** (gain GM-offered trait)
5. **Arc Milestone** (resolve backstory element)

See full details in **Narrative Progression** document.

### After Choosing Advance
- Reset Growth Marks to zero: [ ] [ ] [ ]
- Apply advance immediately
- Update character sheet
- Begin earning marks again

## Pacing Guide

### Per Season (4-5 sessions)
**Expected Advances**: 1-2

### Per Campaign (16-20 sessions)
**Expected Advances**: 6-10

### Per Session
**Expected Marks**: 1-2

**Faster pace**: 2-3 marks per session (very intense)
**Slower pace**: 1 mark every other session (calm)

Adjust based on your table's playstyle.

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I get more than 4 marks per session?**
A: No. Maximum 4 (3 standard + 1 bonus).

**Q: Can marks carry over if I earn 4?**
A: Yes. Take your advance (using 3), and 1 mark carries over.

**Q: What if I don't earn any marks?**
A: That's okay. Some sessions are quiet. Average over time.

**Q: Can I save up more than 3 marks before advancing?**
A: You can, but take advances as soon as you hit 3—progression matters.

**Q: What if I disagree with GM on question answer?**
A: Discuss briefly. GM has final say, but should be generous.

**Q: Do I lose marks if my character dies?**
A: No. New character inherits earned marks (GM discretion).

## GM Quick Reference

### Session End Ritual (5-10 min)
1. Call for reflection
2. Read questions aloud
3. Let players consider
4. Go around table
5. Mark Growth Marks
6. Offer advances if earned

### When to Be Generous
- Player clearly demonstrated question
- Borderline case but good roleplay
- Quiet player stepped up
- Character took meaningful risk

### When to Hold Line
- Player didn't engage with character
- Only tactical/mechanical play
- No meaningful choices made
- Trying to game system

**Default to generosity**—reward character engagement.

### Tracking
Keep simple notes:
- Who earned marks this session
- Why (brief note)
- Who's close to advance
- Ideas for advance options

## Philosophy

Growth Marks measure character development, not player success.

They ask:
- Did you play your character authentically?
- Did you make meaningful choices?
- Did you engage with the story emotionally?
- Did you take risks for the narrative?

They're not:
- Participation trophies
- Loot rewards
- Combat bonuses
- Mandatory per session

They're invitations to become someone different through pressure and choice.
