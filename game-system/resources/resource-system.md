# Resource System

## Overview

Fractured uses **category-based resource management** instead of tracking individual items. Resources are abstract pools rated 0-10 that represent supplies critical to survival.

## Core Principles

**Abstracted Inventory**: No individual item tracking
**Narrative Pressure**: Resources drain through story-driven events, not time
**Survival-Critical Focus**: Track only what matters to life, death, and morale

---

## The Seven Resource Categories

### 1. Medical
**What it covers:**
- First aid supplies (bandages, antiseptic, gauze)
- Trauma care (tourniquets, surgical tools, sutures)
- Infection control (antibiotics, antivirals, sterilization)
- Pain management
- Diagnostic tools

**When depleted (0):**
- Infections spread untreated
- Failed healing rolls
- Wounds worsen over time
- Medical recovery impossible
- Characters die from treatable injuries

### 2. Ammo
**What it covers:**
- Ammunition for all firearms
- Weapon maintenance supplies
- Gun oil and cleaning kits
- Spare parts for weapons

**When depleted (0):**
- Forced into melee or stealth-only missions
- -2 to combat rolls (improvised weapons)
- Cannot use firearms at all
- Panic in firefights

### 3. Fuel
**What it covers:**
- Gasoline and diesel
- Propane and butane
- Generator power
- Vehicle operation

**When depleted (0):**
- No vehicle movement
- Generators shut down
- Loss of power, light, heat
- Cannot travel to distant locations

### 4. Food/Water
**What it covers:**
- Hydration (clean water, purification)
- Nutrition (canned goods, dried food, rations)
- Cooking supplies
- Food preservation

**When depleted (0):**
- Stamina/Sanity checks to avoid collapse
- -2 to all physical actions
- Starvation timer begins (3-5 days)
- Morale crashes

### 5. Utility
**What it covers:**
- Tools (hammers, screwdrivers, wrenches)
- Batteries and power cells
- Radios and communication devices
- Rope, tape, and fasteners
- Flashlights and light sources

**When depleted (0):**
- Failed Engineering rolls
- No tech repairs possible
- Traps and systems fail
- Blackouts and communication loss
- -2 to all utility-based actions

### 6. Sanity/Comfort
**What it covers:**
- Morale boosters (music, books, games)
- Sentimental items (photos, mementos)
- Comfort items (blankets, soap, cigarettes)
- Mental resilience supplies
- Small luxuries

**When depleted (0):**
- Morale crashes, despair spreads
- -1 to all Sanity checks
- Temporary flaws may form
- Group cohesion fractures
- Risk of desertion or breakdown

### 7. Construction
**What it covers:**
- Building materials (wood, metal, concrete)
- Fortification supplies (barbed wire, sandbags)
- Structural repair materials
- Defensive installations

**When depleted (0):**
- Shelter becomes vulnerable
- Barricades fail during attacks
- Cannot repair or upgrade base
- Exposed to elements
- Cannot build new structures

---

## Resource Scale

Each resource is tracked on a **0 to 10 scale**:

**10**: Fully stocked, overflowing
**7-9**: Well-supplied, comfortable
**4-6**: Adequate, sustainable
**2-3**: Running low, concerning
**1**: Critical shortage, emergency
**0**: Completely depleted, crisis

**Resources can exceed 10** if overflow systems exist (caches, storage expansions).

## Resource Usage

### Mission Costs

Before a mission, subtract from relevant resource categories based on mission scale:

| Mission Scale | Typical Cost Per Resource |
|--------------|---------------------------|
| Solo (1 person) | -1 |
| Small Team (2-3) | -1 to -2 |
| Full Team (4-6) | -2 to -3 |
| Major Operation | -3 to -4+ |

**Only subtract resources actually used by mission type.**

**Examples:**

**Scavenging Run** (Small Team):
- Fuel: -1 (travel)
- Utility: -1 (tools for entry)
- Food/Water: -1 (rations for trip)

**Combat Mission** (Full Team):
- Ammo: -3 (firefight)
- Medical: -2 (treating injuries)
- Food/Water: -1 (rations)
- Fuel: -2 (vehicle support)

**Base Defense**:
- Ammo: -2 to -4 (depends on intensity)
- Medical: -2 (casualties)
- Construction: -1 (repairs after)

### Resource Gains

Narrative scavenging finds contribute to pools:

| Haul Size | Gain |
|-----------|------|
| Minimal | +1 |
| Standard | +2 |
| Major | +3 |
| Exceptional Cache | +4 to +5 |

**Example:**
> "You find a pharmacy—trauma kits, bandages, antibiotics still on shelves."
> Result: **Medical +3**

**GM distributes gains** based on what was found narratively.

## Condition-Triggered Resource Drain

Resources **don't drain daily**—they decay when needs are neglected.

| Neglected Action | Resource Lost |
|------------------|---------------|
| No food/water run in 2+ missions | Food/Water -2 |
| No medical downtime or resupply | Medical -2 |
| No morale or comfort focus | Sanity/Comfort -1 |
| No shelter fortification | Construction -2 |
| No tool/equipment scavenging | Utility -1 |
| No fuel recovery or rationing | Fuel -2 |
| No ammo salvage or crafting | Ammo -1 to -2 |

**GM tracks** when categories haven't been addressed and applies drain appropriately.

---

## Sustainability Offsets

If players build systems that address needs, drain is reduced or blocked:

| Built System or Asset | Effect |
|----------------------|--------|
| Active farm/water collection | Blocks Food/Water drain |
| Infirmary or medic rotation | Medical drain -1 max |
| Community hall/mental care | Blocks Sanity/Comfort decay |
| Trap lines/walls/base defenses | Construction drain -1 |
| Tool cache/workbench | Blocks Utility drain |
| Fuel stills or solar panels | Fuel drain -1 |
| Reloading press or ammo salvage | Ammo drain -1 |

**Systems must be:**
- Functional (not compromised)
- Maintained
- Not overused
- Actually built (requires time/resources)

## Resource Management in Play

### Tracking

**Simple Method:**
Use paper/digital tracker with 0-10 scale for each category.

**Visual Method:**
Use tokens, beads, or slider for each category.

**Narrative Method:**
GM tracks mentally and describes resource state narratively.

Choose method that fits your table.

### Announcing Resource State

**At mission start:**
> "Food/Water is at 3. Medical at 5. Ammo at 2. If you use ammo heavily this mission, you'll be at 0."

**During missions:**
> "That firefight cost you 2 Ammo. You're now at 0—weapons click empty."

**After missions:**
> "You gain Medical +3 from the pharmacy. You're now at 8."

**Between sessions:**
> "It's been three missions since you focused on food. Food/Water drops to 1. Hunger is setting in."

### Player Resource Decisions

Players choose mission focus:
- **Scavenging missions**: Seek specific resources
- **Combat missions**: Expend resources for objectives
- **Base missions**: Build sustainability systems
- **Rest missions**: Recover but don't gain resources

**Resource scarcity creates meaningful choices.**

## Resource Crises

When resource hits 0, create crisis:

### Medical at 0
**Scene:**
> "She's bleeding out. You have nothing left—no bandages, no antiseptic, nothing. You can try to improvise, but..."

**Options:**
- Improvise (high difficulty roll)
- Sacrifice personal item
- Let them die
- Seek emergency trade

### Ammo at 0
**Scene:**
> "You pull the trigger. Click. Empty. All of you. The infected are still coming."

**Options:**
- Melee combat (dangerous)
- Flee (abandon position)
- Trap improvisation
- Negotiate if human enemies

### Food/Water at 0
**Scene:**
> "Third day without food. Stomachs cramping. Someone's going to collapse soon."

**Options:**
- Risky scavenge (desperate, dangerous)
- Ration further (Stamina checks)
- Make hard choice (who eats if food is found?)
- Negotiate with other faction

### Fuel at 0
**Scene:**
> "Generator sputters and dies. Darkness. Cold. And you're three miles from base with no gas."

**Options:**
- Walk back (exhaustion, danger)
- Siphon from abandoned vehicles
- Stay and hope
- Sacrifice vehicle

### Sanity/Comfort at 0
**Scene:**
> "Days blur together. No music. No hope. No reason. Someone's going to break."

**Options:**
- Organize morale event (takes time)
- Find comfort items (scavenge)
- Group therapy (Diplomacy rolls)
- Let breakdown happen

## Resource Scenarios

### Abundance (7-10 across categories)
- Players feel secure
- Can take risks
- Build sustainability systems
- Help other factions

**GM Response:**
- Introduce external threats to supplies
- Create temptation (trade resources for intel)
- Allow building and planning
- Plant seeds for scarcity

### Scarcity (2-4 across categories)
- Players feel pressure
- Must prioritize missions
- Hard resource choices
- Tension over distribution

**GM Response:**
- Offer resource missions
- Create internal conflict (who gets what?)
- Test resource-based flaws
- Allow clever solutions

### Crisis (0-1 across categories)
- Players desperate
- Survival uncertain
- Moral compromises likely
- Possible group fracture

**GM Response:**
- Make finding resources urgent
- Force hard choices
- Create faction desperation
- Allow dark solutions

## GM Tools

### Mission Cards (Optional)

Pre-design missions with costs/rewards:

**MISSION: Pharmacy Raid**
- Type: Scavenging
- Cost: Fuel -1, Utility -1, Ammo -1 (if combat)
- Reward: Medical +3 (success), +2 (mixed), +1 (failure)
- Risk: Infected presence, structural collapse

**MISSION: Fuel Depot**
- Type: Scavenging
- Cost: Ammo -2, Medical -1 (likely injuries), Food/Water -1
- Reward: Fuel +4 (success), +2 (mixed), +0 (failure)
- Risk: Hostile survivors, trapped area

**MISSION: Base Defense**
- Type: Combat
- Cost: Ammo -3, Medical -2, Construction -1
- Reward: Safety maintained, potential +1 Sanity/Comfort
- Risk: Casualties, barricade damage

## Philosophy

Resources aren't inventory—they're narrative pressure.

They force choices:
- **What do we prioritize?**
- **Who gets what?**
- **How desperate are we?**
- **What are we willing to do?**

They create tension without bookkeeping.

They make survival feel real without being tedious.

And when resources hit 0, they create the moments that define characters.
