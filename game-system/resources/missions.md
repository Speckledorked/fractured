# Missions and Resource Costs

## Overview

Missions in Fractured are resource-driven choices. Every mission costs something—supplies, safety, morality. Players must choose what to pursue and what to risk.

## Mission Structure

### Basic Mission Format

**Mission Type**: Category of mission
**Objective**: What success looks like
**Resource Cost**: What you spend to attempt it
**Potential Reward**: What you might gain
**Risk Factors**: What could go wrong
**Difficulty**: Expected challenge level

---

## Mission Types

### Scavenging Missions
**Purpose**: Gather specific resources
**Duration**: Half day to full day
**Team Size**: 2-4 recommended

**Resource Costs:**
- Fuel: -1 to -2 (travel)
- Food/Water: -1 (rations)
- Ammo: -1 to -2 (if combat expected)
- Utility: -1 (entry tools)

**Potential Rewards:**
- Target resource: +2 to +4
- Bonus finds: +1 to other categories
- Intel or location discovery

**Common Risks:**
- Infected presence
- Hostile survivors
- Structural collapse
- Trapped locations
- Resource already looted

### Combat Missions
**Purpose**: Eliminate threat or protect location
**Duration**: Hours to half day
**Team Size**: 3-6 recommended

**Resource Costs:**
- Ammo: -2 to -4 (engagement intensity)
- Medical: -1 to -3 (casualties)
- Food/Water: -1 (rations)
- Fuel: -1 to -2 (if vehicle support)

**Potential Rewards:**
- Safety restored
- Enemy resources captured
- Tactical advantage gained
- Morale boost (+1 Sanity/Comfort)

**Common Risks:**
- Casualties or deaths
- Reinforcements arrive
- Tactical disadvantage
- Moral weight of killing

### Exploration Missions
**Purpose**: Scout new areas, gather intelligence
**Duration**: Full day to multiple days
**Team Size**: 2-3 recommended

**Resource Costs:**
- Fuel: -2 to -3 (extended travel)
- Food/Water: -2 (extended rations)
- Utility: -1 (navigation tools)
- Ammo: -1 (defensive)

**Potential Rewards:**
- Map expansion
- Safe zone discovery
- Resource location intel
- Survivor contact

**Common Risks:**
- Getting lost
- Running out of fuel/food far from base
- Unknown threats
- Environmental hazards

### Diplomatic Missions
**Purpose**: Negotiate with other factions
**Duration**: Few hours to half day
**Team Size**: 1-3 recommended

**Resource Costs:**
- Fuel: -1 (travel)
- Food/Water: -1 (trade goods or rations)
- Variable: -1 to -2 (trade resources)

**Potential Rewards:**
- Alliance formed
- Trade route established
- Intel gained
- Conflict avoided

**Common Risks:**
- Betrayal or ambush
- Unfavorable terms
- Hostage situations
- Reputation damage

### Rescue Missions
**Purpose**: Save trapped or endangered individuals
**Duration**: Hours to full day
**Team Size**: 3-5 recommended

**Resource Costs:**
- Fuel: -1 to -2 (rapid response)
- Ammo: -2 to -3 (likely combat)
- Medical: -2 to -3 (treating rescued/injured)
- Utility: -1 (extraction tools)

**Potential Rewards:**
- New group members
- Moral boost (+2 Sanity/Comfort)
- Reputation gain
- Rescued person's skills/resources

**Common Risks:**
- Trap or ambush
- Rescue target already dead/turned
- Extraction failure
- Heavy casualties

### Construction/Repair Missions
**Purpose**: Build or repair base infrastructure
**Duration**: Half day to multiple days
**Team Size**: 2-4 recommended

**Resource Costs:**
- Construction: -2 to -4 (materials)
- Utility: -1 to -2 (tools)
- Food/Water: -1 per day (labor rations)

**Potential Rewards:**
- Sustainability system built
- Barricades strengthened
- Living conditions improved
- Resource drain reduced

**Common Risks:**
- Structural collapse during work
- Attacks during construction
- Resource shortage mid-project
- Design failures

## Mission Cost Scaling

### Solo Mission (1 person)
**Base Costs**: -1 per relevant resource
**Efficiency**: Low resource use
**Risk**: High personal danger
**Use when**: Stealth, speed, or desperate

**Example:**
Solo scavenge: Fuel -1, Food -1, Utility -1, Ammo -1 if needed

### Small Team (2-3 people)
**Base Costs**: -1 to -2 per relevant resource
**Efficiency**: Balanced
**Risk**: Moderate
**Use when**: Standard operations

**Example:**
Small team pharmacy raid: Fuel -1, Utility -1, Ammo -2, Food -1

### Full Team (4-6 people)
**Base Costs**: -2 to -3 per relevant resource
**Efficiency**: High capability but high cost
**Risk**: Lower per person, higher total
**Use when**: Major operations, combat, rescue

**Example:**
Full team assault: Ammo -3, Medical -2, Food -2, Fuel -2

### Major Operation (Entire group + resources)
**Base Costs**: -3 to -5 per relevant resource
**Efficiency**: All-in
**Risk**: Everything at stake
**Use when**: Finale, desperate gambit, season climax

**Example:**
Final evacuation attempt: All resources -3 minimum

## Mission Examples

- Fuel: -1 (travel)

**Objective**: Defend captured fuel depot from raiders

- Ammo: -3 (firefight)

- Fuel: +0 (preserved existing supply)

- Intel on safe routes

- Caravan is hostile

## Mission Planning

### Pre-Mission Decisions

**1. Team Selection**
- Who goes? Who stays?
- Match skills to mission type
- Consider Crisis Profiles under pressure
- Balance risk vs capability

**2. Resource Allocation**
- What can we afford to spend?
- What resources are critical?
- Can we handle costs if things go wrong?
- Do we have sustainability systems to offset?

**3. Risk Assessment**
- What's the worst that could happen?
- Are we prepared for casualties?
- What's our backup plan?
- When do we abort?

**4. Success Criteria**
- What does success look like?
- What's minimum acceptable outcome?
- When do we cut losses?
- What's non-negotiable?

### Mission Execution

**During Mission:**
- GM describes situation
- Players declare actions
- Rolls determine outcomes
- Resources spent as narrative demands
- Complications arise from failed/mixed rolls
- Red Line moments may emerge

**Post-Mission:**
- Calculate final resource costs
- Apply gains from success
- Address injuries/trauma
- Debrief and process
- Plan next move

## Resource Mission Priority Matrix

Use this to help players decide mission focus:

### Critical Priority (Resource at 0-1)
**Must address immediately:**
- Food/Water: Starvation imminent
- Medical: Wounded dying
- Ammo: Defenseless

**Suggested missions:**
- Emergency scavenge (desperate, risky)
- Faction trade (unfavorable terms acceptable)
- High-risk raid

### High Priority (Resource at 2-3)
**Address soon:**
- Any combat resource low
- Sustainability system breaking
- Base vulnerability

**Suggested missions:**
- Standard scavenging
- Negotiated trade
- Moderate-risk operations

### Medium Priority (Resource at 4-6)
**Address when able:**
- Comfort and utility
- Stockpiling for future
- Building sustainability

**Suggested missions:**
- Exploration for future sites
- Diplomatic relationship building
- Base construction projects

### Low Priority (Resource at 7-10)
**Optional:**
- Excess stockpiling
- Helping other factions
- Long-term projects

**Suggested missions:**
- Ambitious exploration
- Altruistic rescue
- Community building

## GM Mission Design

### Creating Custom Missions

**1. Establish Need**
What resource or objective drives this mission?

**2. Set Location**
Where is the mission? What's there narratively?

**3. Determine Costs**
What will attempting this cost in resources?

**4. Define Rewards**
What can success provide? Be specific.

**5. Add Complications**
What makes this interesting/dangerous?

**6. Create Decision Points**
Where do players make meaningful choices?

### Mission Hooks

**Environmental:**
- "Smoke rises from the medical complex"
- "Radio picks up distress call"
- "Infected herd moving toward fuel depot"

**NPC-Driven:**
- "Faction leader offers trade: food for fuel"
- "Scout reports cache in dangerous zone"
- "Survivor begs for rescue of family"

**Resource-Driven:**
- "Medical down to 1—someone will die soon"
- "Ammo at 0—you're defenseless"
- "No food in two days—hunger sets in"

**Character-Driven:**
- "Your Red Line is tested by this mission"
- "Your flaw makes this mission personal"
- "Your arc connects to this location"

## Sustainability Through Missions

### Building Systems

Some missions create ongoing benefits:

**Water Purification System**
- Mission: Scavenge + install filtration
- Cost: Construction -3, Utility -2
- Benefit: Food/Water drain blocked permanently

**Fortified Perimeter**
- Mission: Build + reinforce walls
- Cost: Construction -4, Utility -1
- Benefit: Construction drain -1, defense bonus

**Ammo Reloading Station**
- Mission: Find press + supplies
- Cost: Utility -2, Ammo -1 (to start)
- Benefit: Ammo drain -1, can craft limited ammo

**Community Garden**
- Mission: Clear land + plant + maintain
- Cost: Construction -2, Utility -1, time
- Benefit: Food/Water drain blocked after growth

### Long-Term Investment

Missions that reduce future resource costs:
- Building sustainability systems
- Establishing trade routes
- Securing safe scavenging zones
- Training specialists

**These missions cost heavily up front but pay off over campaign.**

## Philosophy

Missions aren't just things to do—they're choices that matter.

Every mission asks:
- **What are we willing to spend?**
- **What are we willing to risk?**
- **Who do we send into danger?**
- **What happens if we fail?**

Resource costs make those questions real.

And when resources are low, missions become desperate gambles.

That's when character moments emerge.
