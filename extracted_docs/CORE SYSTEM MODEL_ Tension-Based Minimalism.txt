🔹 1. STAT TRIGGER ROLLS 

Stats = Narrative triggers, not hard math


When you do something risky that fits a core stat, you roll a d6 or narrative coin flip


Example:

Roll outcomes:

6 = Full success


4–5 = Success with cost


1–3 = Failure or moral strain

 (Modifiers optional based on traits)


🎲 STAT TRIGGER EXAMPLES

🪓 SURVIVAL

When you act on instinct in dangerous terrain or unknown conditions.

Examples:

You’re crawling through a half-collapsed tunnel and hear something breathing.


You’re foraging at night and hear glass crack nearby.


You try to bypass a frozen river without testing the ice.


You’re low on food and gamble on eating something you think is edible.

 → Roll Survival. (Success = You adapt. Failure = Injury, exposure, misstep.)


🧠 SANITY

When you face horror, grief, or the unnatural.

Examples:

You discover a loved one turned, still clutching something meaningful.


You’re the only one awake during a long watch, and something starts whispering your name.


A decision you made costs someone their life, and the others are silent.


You see something moving in a mirror—but not in the room.

 → Roll Sanity. (Success = You anchor. Failure = Spiral, freeze, lash out.)


🏃‍♂️ STAMINA

When you push your body past safe limits.

Examples:

You’re sprinting full-out with someone on your back while being chased.


You’ve been awake for 36 hours and are told to take another shift.


You volunteer to carry the heavy water drum uphill.


You refuse medical rest to keep someone else alive.

 → Roll Stamina. (Success = You endure. Failure = Collapse, injury, hallucination.)


🫂 EMPATHY

When you try to connect, calm, or rescue another emotionally.

Examples:

A kid is panicking and might bolt into danger.


A crewmate is curled up and won’t speak after a mission goes wrong.


A stranger holds a knife to their own throat, sobbing.


Your friend just crossed their Red Line and can’t look at you.

 → Roll Empathy. (Success = You reach them. Failure = They break… or you do.)


🪨 RESOLVE

When you hold your line in moral or personal conflict.

Examples:

Everyone agrees to abandon someone—you don’t.


You’re asked to lie to protect morale but it eats at you.


You could steal medicine from another group.


You’re told to break your Red Line or be exiled.

 → Roll Resolve. (Success = You stay whole. Failure = Fracture, shame, compromise.)

🔹 2. TRAITS + FLAWS ARE TRIGGERS

Instead of bonuses, traits create complication hooks:

“If your trait applies, you get an edge… but it may backfire.”


“If your flaw triggers, GM can ask you to roll even if you weren’t planning to.”


E.g.:

Trait: “First In, Last Out”

➤ GM: “The rest of your group is hesitating. You’re not. Roll Survival with pressure.”

🔹 3. NO INITIATIVE, JUST ESCALATION

Use “Pressure Tokens” or “Scene Tension Meters” instead of turns


GM/World reacts to:


Hesitation


Noise


Violence


Moral fracture


Think a quiet firefight becoming a noise trap becoming a base breach.

🔹 4. CONSEQUENCES ARE NARRATIVE, NOT NUMERIC

You don’t lose “HP”—you lose:


Trust


Morale


Time


A Red Line


The real cost of failure is what it forces you to become.

Stat | Roll Trigger

Survival | When you act on instinct in a dangerous place

Sanity | When you face something mentally shattering

Stamina | When you push your body past safe limits

Empathy | When you try to connect or de-escalate

Resolve | When you stand firm in moral conflict
