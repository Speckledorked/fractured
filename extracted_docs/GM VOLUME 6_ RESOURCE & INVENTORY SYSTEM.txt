VOLUME 6: RESOURCE & INVENTORY SYSTEM

Category-Based Survival Management for Narrative-Driven Play

🔹 CORE PRINCIPLES

Abstracted Inventory: No individual item tracking


Narrative Pressure: Resources drain only through story-driven events


Survival-Critical Focus: Track only what matters to life, death, and morale


📅 RESOURCE CATEGORIES

📊 RESOURCE SCALE

Each resource is tracked on a scale of 0 to 10:

0 = Critical Shortage (penalties triggered)


10 = Fully Stocked


Resources may exceed 10 if overflow systems (e.g., caches) exist.

🔄 RESOURCE USAGE

✅ MISSION COSTS

Before a mission, subtract from relevant resource categories:

Only subtract from the resources actually used by the mission type.

➕ RESOURCE GAINS

Narrative scavenging finds contribute to general pools:

Example: Finding trauma kits, bandages, and antiseptic = +3 Medical

🕗 CONDITION-TRIGGERED RESOURCE DRAIN

Resources are not drained daily. Instead, they decay when the group neglects key needs for too long.

🧱 SUSTAINABILITY OFFSETS

If players have built systems that address a need, that drain is reduced or blocked.

GM Tip: Systems must be functional, not compromised, and not overused to apply offsets.

❌ RESOURCE AT 0: CONSEQUENCES

✨ OPTIONAL GM TOOLS

Mission Cards: Prebuilt missions with projected cost/reward


Resource Tracker: Printable bar cards or dry-erase sliders


Crisis Flags: Tokens that mark when 0-resource state is reached


Sabotage/Decay Events: Narrative prompts to simulate system breakdown


This system keeps your survival world intense, fast-moving, and grounded—without ever needing a daily ledger.

Category | Covers...

Medical | First aid, trauma care, infection control

Ammo | Ammunition, weapon maintenance

Fuel | Gas, diesel, propane, generator power

Food/Water | Hydration, nutrition, ration storage

Utility | Tools, batteries, radios, rope, flashlights

Sanity/Comfort | Morale boosts, sentimentals, mental resilience

Construction | Building materials, fortifications, structural repair

Mission Scale | Typical Cost Per Resource

Solo (1 person) | −1

Small Team (2–3) | −1 to −2

Full Team (4–6) | −2 to −3

Major Operation | −3 to −4+

Haul Size | Gain

Minimal | +1

Standard | +2

Major | +3

Exceptional Cache | +4 to +5

Neglected Action | Resource Lost

No food/water run in 2+ missions | −2 Food/Water

No medical downtime or resupply | −2 Medical

No morale or comfort focus | −1 Sanity/Comfort

No shelter fortification | −2 Construction

No tool/equipment scavenging | −1 Utility

No fuel recovery or rationing | −2 Fuel

No ammo salvage or crafting | −1 to −2 Ammo

Built System or Asset | Negates or Reduces...

Active farm/water collection | Blocks Food/Water drain

Infirmary or medic rotation | −1 max Medical drain

Community hall/mental care | Blocks Sanity/Comfort decay

Trap lines/walls/base defenses | −1 Construction decay

Tool cache/workbench | Blocks Utility drain

Fuel stills or solar panels | −1 Fuel drain

Reloading press or ammo salvage | −1 Ammo drain

Resource | Effect When Depleted

Medical | Infections, failed healing, wounds worsen

Ammo | Forced into melee or stealth-only missions

Fuel | No movement, generators shut down

Food/Water | Stamina/Sanity checks to avoid collapse

Utility | Failed traps, tech failures, blackouts

Sanity/Comfort | Morale crashes, temporary flaws may form

Construction | Shelter vulnerable, barricades fail
