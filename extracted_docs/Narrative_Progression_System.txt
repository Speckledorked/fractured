📈 Narrative Progression System

Overview

This progression system is designed to reward emotional depth, meaningful decisions, and long-term character development. It replaces traditional XP grinding with a reflective, narrative-based model that reinforces player growth and story impact.

🎯 Growth Marks

At the end of each session, the GM leads a short reflection. Players answer a set of questions based on their actions, decisions, and development. If a player can honestly answer 'yes' they earn 1 Growth Mark.

Once a player collects 3 Growth Marks, they unlock a Character Advance.

✅ Session Reflection Questions

Ask the following questions aloud at the end of each session:

1. Did you act in accordance with your traits, flaws, or moral code—even when it cost you something?

2. Did you influence another character in a meaningful way—through trust, conflict, loyalty, or sacrifice?

3. Did you face a fear, hardship, or moral choice that tested your values—and grow from it, even slightly?

4. (Optional Bonus) Did you take a personal risk for someone else’s survival, well-being, or hope?

✅ If the player answers 'yes' they earn 1 Growth Mark.

🪜 Character Advances (Every 3 Growth Marks)

Once a player has earned 3 Growth Marks, they choose one of the following advance options:

🔹 Stat Improvement – Raise one stat by +1, up to a max of 5. Must be justified through recent in-game experience.

🔹 Trait Evolution – Upgrade one existing trait into a stronger or more refined version based on its narrative arc.

🔹 Flaw Breakthrough or Transformation – Resolve a flaw or replace it with a more evolved version (e.g. Savior Syndrome → Resentful Savior). Must be supported by in-character growth or emotional conflict.

🔹 New Trait Unlock – Gain a new Conditional or Latent Trait offered by the GM, based on your choices and arc.

🔹 Arc Milestone – Instead of a mechanical bonus, resolve a major backstory thread, mystery, or personal mission. This may unlock future bonuses or reputation impact.

🧠 Optional GM Tools

To enhance and guide the progression system, GMs may also consider:

Keeping a Growth Log for each player (3-slot tracker; reset on Advance).

Allowing players to nominate one other player each session for a bonus Growth Mark based on emotional impact or breakthrough moments.

Granting bonus Growth Marks for 'Red Line Moments'—scenes where a character hits a personal or moral breaking point and leans into it.

Tying specific trait evolutions to story events, milestones, or faction recognition.
