📖 Format per Entry

Each Trait or Flaw will follow this structure:

✦ Name

Type: (Core | Conditional | Latent | Passive | Situational)
 Description: 1–2 sentence narrative impact
 Gameplay Effect: Bonus, penalty, or behavior effect
 Triggers / Risk Factors: Conditions where the trait may help or hinder
 Evolution Path (optional): How it might grow, worsen, or transform

🧠 PSYCHOLOGICAL TRAITS 

✦ Unshakable

Type: Core
 Description: Rarely cracks under pressure. Calm, even in chaos.
 Gameplay Effect: +1 to Sanity checks during trauma or horror events.
 Triggers / Risk Factors: May struggle to emotionally connect.
 Evolution Path: “Stoic Guardian” or “Emotional Bottleneck”

✦ Hyperfocused

Type: Conditional
 Description: Can lock in on one problem and burn through it obsessively.
 Gameplay Effect: +2 on single-task actions, -1 to peripheral awareness.
 Triggers / Risk Factors: May ignore team needs.
 Evolution Path: “Master Technician” or “Tunnel Vision”

✦ Optimist

Type: Passive
 Description: Always finds a silver lining, even when there shouldn’t be one.
 Gameplay Effect: Reduces group despair penalties by 1 in loss scenarios.
 Triggers / Risk Factors: May become naive or unprepared.
 Evolution Path: “Hope-Bearer” or “Broken Idealist”

✦ Paranoid

Type: Situational
 Description: Sees threats where others don’t—but is right more often than not.
 Gameplay Effect: Can detect hidden agendas or traps (+1) at the cost of social friction.
 Triggers / Risk Factors: Trust issues, isolation, false positives.
 Evolution Path: “Dark Prophet” or “Saboteur”

✦ Calculating

Type: Core
 Description: Weighs every action. Nothing is random.
 Gameplay Effect: Gains reroll once per day on tactical, planning, or resource-based checks.
 Triggers / Risk Factors: Hesitates in spontaneous situations.
 Evolution Path: “Strategic Mastermind” or “Emotionless Planner”

✦ Curious

Type: Core
 Description: Must know how things work. Opens every door.
 Gameplay Effect: Bonus clue detection or secret finding (+1).
 Triggers / Risk Factors: Risk-prone. May break protocol to investigate.
 Evolution Path: “Field Scholar” or “Unleashed Catalyst”

✦ Mindful

Type: Passive
 Description: Practices emotional and sensory awareness. Breathes, observes, responds.
 Gameplay Effect: +1 to recovery checks after mental trauma or strain.
 Triggers / Risk Factors: May pause too long in high-pressure sequences.
 Evolution Path: “Centered Guardian” or “Detached Observer”

✦ Adaptive Thinker

Type: Core
 Description: Thinks fast. Shifts strategies mid-move.
 Gameplay Effect: Ignore -1 penalties from surprise, darkness, or sudden chaos.
 Triggers / Risk Factors: May clash with rigid planners.
 Evolution Path: “Flow Operator” or “Rogue Agent”

✦ Pattern Seeker

Type: Conditional
 Description: Notices trends others miss. Draws connections no one else sees.
 Gameplay Effect: Bonus insight into enemy behavior, movement, or spread.
 Triggers / Risk Factors: May interpret noise as signal.
 Evolution Path: “Oracle” or “Conspiracist”

✦ Detached

Type: Passive
 Description: Keeps a healthy emotional distance to protect themselves.
 Gameplay Effect: Immune to manipulation, guilt, or emotional coercion.
 Triggers / Risk Factors: Reduced empathy, may seem cold.
 Evolution Path: “Tactician” or “Isolate”

✦ Driven

Type: Core
 Description: Has a purpose. It defines them.
 Gameplay Effect: +1 to complete long-term personal goals.
 Triggers / Risk Factors: Burns out if goal becomes impossible.
 Evolution Path: “Force of Will” or “Obsessive Pursuer”

✦ Calculated Risk-Taker

Type: Tactical
 Description: Doesn’t shy away from risk—but never leaps blind.
 Gameplay Effect: Reroll 1 failed attempt per day where odds are against them.
 Triggers / Risk Factors: May still take unjustified risks for gain.
 Evolution Path: “Heroic Operator” or “Broken Gambler”

✦ Restless Mind

Type: Latent
 Description: Never stops thinking. Always moving from one idea to the next.
 Gameplay Effect: Gains insight rolls faster, but suffers penalties on sustained tasks.
 Triggers / Risk Factors: Fatigue, team frustration.
 Evolution Path: “Innovator” or “Mental Spiral”

✦ Overprepared

Type: Core
 Description: Plans for every possibility. Then adds one more.
 Gameplay Effect: +1 to resource planning, pre-mission loadouts.
 Triggers / Risk Factors: Suffers under improvisation or chaos.
 Evolution Path: “Quartermaster” or “Frozen Planner”

✦ Survivor's Grit

Type: Passive
 Description: Has already been through hell. And came out stronger.
 Gameplay Effect: +1 on all Willpower/Endurance rolls when solo.
 Triggers / Risk Factors: May default to isolation.
 Evolution Path: “Iron Willed” or “Burned Out”

✦ Lucid Dreamer

Type: Unique
 Description: Sees visions. Maybe future. Maybe past.
 Gameplay Effect: Can occasionally receive GM-fed glimpses or intuitive insights.
 Triggers / Risk Factors: Player cannot always control when it happens.
 Evolution Path: “Seer” or “Fractured Mind”

✦ Introspective

Type: Passive
 Description: Turns inward often. Reflects deeply.
 Gameplay Effect: Gains narrative XP faster from personal failures.
 Triggers / Risk Factors: Risk of emotional withdrawal.
 Evolution Path: “Sage” or “Self-Doubt Spiral”

✦ Compartmentalizer

Type: Conditional
 Description: Locks away trauma until it’s convenient to feel it.
 Gameplay Effect: No penalty for trauma mid-mission; doubles post-mission impact.
 Triggers / Risk Factors: Long-term mental collapse risk.
 Evolution Path: “Unbreakable Front” or “Delayed Breakdown”

✦ Focused Under Fire

Type: Core
 Description: Stress makes them better.
 Gameplay Effect: +1 to actions while wounded, scared, or under time limit.
 Triggers / Risk Factors: May seek danger subconsciously.
 Evolution Path: “Combat Tactician” or “Adrenaline Addict”

✦ Internal Compass

Type: Moral
 Description: Has a clear inner code—and follows it no matter what.
 Gameplay Effect: Gains reroll on any choice-related moral crisis.
 Triggers / Risk Factors: Cannot compromise easily.
 Evolution Path: “Pillar” or “Broken Zealot”
