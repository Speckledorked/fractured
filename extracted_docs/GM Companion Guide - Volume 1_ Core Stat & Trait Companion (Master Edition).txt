2w🧩 SECTION 6: Conflict Profile Builder

"Who do they become under pressure—and who are they afraid of becoming?"

This section gives the GM a toolkit to assemble a unique conflict identity for each character based on their full quiz. Rather than slotting players into archetypes, you’ll build a behavioral fingerprint made of 5 key elements:

🧱 1. Instinctive Response Pattern

How does the character behave when confronted with stress, danger, or decision-making?

Draw from Sections D & C

GM Tip: Assign a keyword that defines how they instinctively handle crisis (e.g., Protect, Withdraw, Intervene, Control).

🪞 2. Emotional Anchor

What belief, value, or feeling grounds them?

Draw from Sections B, C & E

GM Tip: Create a trigger where this belief is challenged or violated.

🩸 3. Moral Threshold (Line They Won’t Cross… Yet)

Where do they draw the line?

Draw from Section C

GM Tip: Write a line in their file:

“Will fracture if forced to _______.”

🧬 4. Hidden Driver or Fear

What motivates or terrifies them at the core?

Draw from Sections E (Q81–100), esp. Q86, Q94, Q100

GM Tip: Consider tying this to a mystery, secret, or personal side-quest. You now have leverage.

🎭 5. Crisis Persona (Who they become when pushed)

This is not a role—they evolve into it during the story.

Create a short, evolving label:

“The Unyielding Protector” – Always puts others first, even when it breaks them.
 “The Quiet Flame” – Withdraws under pressure until they explode.
 “The Smiling Knife” – Charming, trusted… but capable of brutality.
 “The Ghost with Guilt” – Detached until the past drags them back.
 “The One Who Runs” – Reliable… until the stakes are too personal.

GM Tip: Don’t tell the player this title. Use it as a GM-only development arc guide.

🎯 How to Use the Conflict Profile Builder

During prep or Session Zero:

Highlight responses across Sections C–E

Use this profile to:

Give every player a story arc worth surviving
