🎲 Action Cycle & Risk Resolution System

⚙️ ACTION CYCLE (Narrative-Driven, Freeform)

Play flows through narrative beats, not turns. These are called Crisis Frames—scenes where danger, time pressure, or moral weight shift the action.

Each player acts when it makes sense narratively.

- If their action could fail, trigger risk, or cost something, the GM calls for a roll.
- No initiative. No turn order.
- GM escalates tension through fiction or a Threat Clock if players hesitate or fail.

🎲 RISK ROLL SYSTEM

Whenever a player attempts something risky, roll:

2d6 + Relevant Stat

Choose the stat that matches what the player is trying to do (freeform):

Combat – attacking, defending, intimidation

Survival – navigation, evasion, foraging, adaptation

Diplomacy – persuasion, empathy, negotiation

Engineering – building, repairing, modifying

Medical – triage, healing, stabilizing

Stamina – pushing limits, enduring pain/fatigue

Sanity – resisting fear, keeping composure

Health – shrugging injury, physical resistance

🎯 ROLL OUTCOMES (Narrative Resolution Scale)


On a 7–9, the GM chooses or offers consequences:

You draw attention

You succeed but something breaks, is delayed, or exposed

You leave yourself or someone else vulnerable

You succeed… but pay for it later


On a 6–, the GM responds with:

Immediate danger escalation

Threat Clock advancement

Resource loss

Trait/Flaw trigger

Moral complication

Consequence that snowballs

🧮 MODIFIERS

⏳ OPTIONAL: THREAT CLOCK

Use a 4- or 6-segment Threat Clock to track how close the scene is to collapse.

Advance the clock when:


Players fail a roll

Players hesitate or stall

Players take noisy/desperate action


At full clock, unleash a GM escalation (walker breach, structure collapse, betrayal, panic, etc.)

Total | Result | Interpretation

10+ | Success | You achieve your goal cleanly, with no complication.

7–9 | Mixed Success | You succeed, but at a cost. The GM offers 1–2 hard options.

6 or less | Failure | The action fails—or succeeds in the worst possible way. The GM makes a hard move.

Situation | Modifier

Relevant Trait or Training | +1

Significant teammate aid | +1

Active Condition or Flaw | –1

No tools/resources | –1

Severe environmental stress | –1 to –2
