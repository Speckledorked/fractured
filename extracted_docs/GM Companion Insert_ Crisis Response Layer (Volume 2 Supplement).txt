🎭 GM Companion Insert: Crisis Response Layer (Volume 2 Supplement)

Purpose: Use this sheet as a fast-reference guide to identify and manage evolving character behavior under pressure—without tracking full emotional engines.

🔄 Crisis Behavior Tags

Attach these tags to any Trait or Flaw to describe how it interacts with pressure, trauma, and group dynamics.

🎭 Trait/Flaw Evolution Paths (Expanded Use)

Each trait/flaw now includes:

Spiral Path: How it decays under prolonged stress.


Resilience Path: How it refines into a defining strength.


Group Effect (optional): How others react to their evolution or collapse.


Example:
 “Soul Anchor” may become dangerously possessive under trauma (🕳), or emerge as a heroic protector when tempered (🌱).

🧠 GM Prompts at Key Moments

Use the following prompts when a character:

Fails a high-stakes roll tied to trauma, morality, or personal bonds


Endures extended stress conditions (starvation, moral conflict, repeated loss)


Reaches a major arc milestone


Ask yourself:

Is this trait intensifying? Mutating? Breaking?


Is this flaw starting to define their arc?


Is this the moment they evolve—or fracture?


💡 Integration Suggestions

Tag traits during Session Zero or GM prep


Apply tags dynamically as character arcs evolve


Use in tension scenes, downtime reflection, or group conflict


Combine with Volume 1’s Player Personality-Adaptive System for deeper synergy


Tag | Meaning

🔺 Stress Reactive | The trait or flaw mutates under emotional or situational pressure.

⚖️ Group Modifier | Impacts team morale, cohesion, or leadership dynamics.

🕳 Spiral Risk | May degrade into worse behaviors if ignored (e.g. trauma loop, burnout).

🌱 Resilience Arc | Can evolve into a powerful strength after being tested.
