🌍 REVISED FOUR-SEASON STORY SPINE

A tighter, harder-hitting, GM-free arc for 1–4 players

🟥 Season 1: The Break

*You thought you'd have time. You were wrong.*

📣 Collapse Event:

The outbreak explodes without warning. Shelters fail. Authorities vanish. Power, roads, and order dissolve overnight.

🎯 Narrative Pressure:

• Choose what to carry—and who to leave.

• First infected contact (close, personal, overwhelming).

• Refugees surge. Supplies dwindle by the hour.

💥 Key Gameplay Beats:

• First Red Line moment

• Emotional anchors shatter

• Rapid fort or base setup under pressure

🔥 Red Line Prompt:

> You have five minutes. Who do you go back for?

⚫ Season 2: The Divide

*Now it's just you... and whoever made it through.*

📣 Collapse Event:

Infection spreads faster than rumor. Group splits begin—some people can’t handle the weight. Small factions form, each with different ethics.

🎯 Narrative Pressure:

• Decide: fortify, flee, or scavenge.

• Rationing begins. Trust starts cracking.

• Internal conflict and loss shape the party.

💥 Key Gameplay Beats:

• Trait evolutions and flaw flare-ups

• Scene hooks from 'Who eats first?' to 'You lied to keep the peace'

• Base becomes a symbol of either hope… or cage

🔥 Red Line Prompt:

> Someone has to go. Who decides—and why is it you?

🌑 Season 3: The Fall

*The safe places are gone. So are the safe people.*

📣 Collapse Event:

A herd sweeps through—wipes out known safe zones. NPC betrayal, death, or madness fractures what’s left. Morale crashes. Sanity rolls trigger more often.

🎯 Narrative Pressure:

• Make brutal choices: mercy kills, deserter hunts, self-sacrifice

• Confront your flaws: they’re not quirks anymore—they’re threats

• Pressure rises from both inside and out

💥 Key Gameplay Beats:

• Final trait evolutions or irreversible flaw mutations

• Abandon or transform the base

• Personal losses escalate

🔥 Red Line Prompt:

> What part of yourself do you kill so the rest can live?

🟨 Season 4: The Reckoning

*You made it here. That doesn’t mean you survived it.*

📣 Collapse Event:

A final, world-shifting decision: Escape attempt, Final stand, Collapse into the unknown. Group unity either shatters or crystallizes. All arcs resolve—legacy, guilt, betrayal, love.

🎯 Narrative Pressure:

• Choose who you are now

• Face your Red Line directly

• End something permanent

💥 Key Gameplay Beats:

• Epilogue triggers: death, exile, memory

• Last resource run, last betrayal, last mercy

• Resolution = scars, not success

🔥 Red Line Prompt:

> To live… what would you let yourself become?
